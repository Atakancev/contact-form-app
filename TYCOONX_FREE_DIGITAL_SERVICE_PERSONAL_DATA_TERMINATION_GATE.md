# TycoonX Free Digital Service, Personal Data & Termination Release Gate

Last reviewed: September 1, 2026
Owner: CK-Labs
Scope: the free TycoonX service, free/promotional/test grants, privacy-right exercises, account closure, permanent service discontinuation, and retrieval of user-created content after termination under German/EU digital-product law.

## Purpose

TycoonX is free to access at its base layer and also offers one-time paid products such as Diamonds, 30-Day VIP, and limited-window Lifetime VIP. A free price does **not** automatically mean that German/EU digital-product consumer law is irrelevant.

This gate prevents two opposite mistakes:

1. treating every free TycoonX account as if no consumer digital-product rights can ever apply; and
2. treating personal data as a monetary commodity or assuming that every item of data automatically brings the whole free service inside the same statutory regime.

The correct approach is transaction- and processing-specific. GDPR and other data-protection law remain separate and prevail where they conflict with digital-contract rules.

This gate supplements, but does not replace, the TycoonX Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Purchases & Refunds Policy, Community Standards, Account Deletion Release Gate, and mandatory consumer law.

## 1. German BGB § 327(3): a free digital service can still be in scope

German BGB § 327(1) applies the digital-product rules to consumer contracts for digital content or digital services supplied against payment of a price.

BGB § 327(3) extends that framework to certain contracts where the consumer **provides or undertakes to provide personal data**, unless the exception in BGB § 312(1a) sentence 2 applies.

For TycoonX, never use the shortcut:

> “The base game is free, so §§ 327 et seq. BGB can never apply.”

Instead, create a dated classification for the actual free-service processing model.

### The BGB § 312(1a) exception matters

BGB § 312(1a) sentence 2 excludes the personal-data extension where the trader processes the consumer-provided personal data **exclusively** to perform the trader's contractual obligations or comply with legal requirements and for no other purpose.

Examples:

- an account identifier used only to authenticate the player and maintain the requested persistent game state may fall within the statutory exception if it is genuinely processed only for service performance and legal compliance;
- data additionally used for optional analytics, advertising, behavioral profiling, marketing, or another non-essential purpose can change the legal analysis;
- the fact that a privacy policy lists a processing purpose does not itself decide the consumer-contract classification. The real production processing and the actual contract must be mapped.

Do not call personal data the “price” of TycoonX in player-facing prose. Directive (EU) 2019/770 extends contractual remedies to qualifying data-based business models without turning the fundamental right to personal-data protection into a tradable commodity.

## 2. Directive (EU) 2019/770 baseline

Directive (EU) 2019/770 covers qualifying digital-content and digital-service contracts where the consumer pays a price and also qualifying cases where the consumer provides or undertakes to provide personal data, subject to the Directive's exceptions.

The European Commission currently explains that consumers can have remedies for faulty digital content or services regardless of whether they paid money or only provided personal data in a qualifying case.

For TycoonX this means that, where the statutory scope applies, free-service users can still have mandatory rights concerning matters such as:

- supply;
- conformity;
- required updates;
- cure;
- termination;
- certain modifications; and
- post-termination handling of content.

Do not weaken the TycoonX support model to “no purchase means no remedy.”

## 3. Free base service is different from a free promotional grant

Keep these categories separate:

### Free TycoonX base service

The continuing game service can itself be a digital service. Its legal classification depends on the actual contract and data-processing model.

### Promotional or goodwill Diamonds

Free, event, compensation, tester, review, or goodwill Diamonds can have clearly disclosed conditions and may differ from purchased Diamonds. They do not automatically become a cash claim or create a right to equivalent future grants.

However, “free” does not authorize CK-Labs to ignore any mandatory rights that attach to the relevant consumer contract.

### Free/promotional VIP

A complimentary 30-Day VIP period or other free VIP grant is not a paid purchase merely because a paid version also exists. Do not fabricate a provider refund for a zero-price grant.

If the free grant forms part of a qualifying digital-product consumer contract, applicable conformity or termination rules still require separate analysis.

### Digital gifts and BGB § 516a

German BGB § 516a provides a special rule for consumer contracts gifting digital products where the consumer provides personal data within BGB § 327(3). Do not assume every in-game bonus is a separate gift contract, but do not assume the word “gift” automatically removes digital-product protections either.

## 4. BGB § 327q: exercising privacy rights does not automatically cancel the contract

This is a critical TycoonX privacy/contract separation rule.

Under BGB § 327q(1), the exercise of data-subject rights and privacy declarations after conclusion of the contract does **not** by itself affect the validity of the contract.

Examples:

- withdrawing optional analytics consent must not automatically delete the TycoonX account;
- objecting to eligible legitimate-interest processing must not automatically revoke purchased Diamonds;
- a GDPR access or erasure request must not automatically become a chargeback, fraud flag, or moderation violation;
- refusing optional marketing must not shorten 30-Day VIP or revoke Lifetime VIP.

The privacy request and the contractual entitlement state are separate workflows.

## 5. BGB § 327q(2): termination after consent withdrawal or objection is narrow

For a contract requiring a series of digital-product supplies or continuing supply, BGB § 327q(2) can allow the trader to terminate without notice after the consumer withdraws consent or objects to further processing **only where continuing the contract is unreasonable**, taking into account:

- the personal-data processing that remains lawfully available after the privacy declaration; and
- the interests of both parties.

Operational rule for TycoonX:

- first determine which processing actually stopped;
- determine which separate legal bases remain available;
- determine whether the affected processing is genuinely necessary for continued provision of the relevant service;
- consider whether a narrower feature restriction can solve the issue;
- do not use the privacy request as a pretext to terminate unrelated paid entitlements or punish the player.

A marketing-consent withdrawal should normally disable the marketing use, not the game account.

If an account identifier necessary to provide persistent account access can no longer lawfully be processed and continued supply genuinely becomes unreasonable, document the specific legal/technical reason before termination.

## 6. BGB § 327q(3): no damages because the consumer exercised privacy rights

BGB § 327q(3) excludes compensation claims by the trader against the consumer merely because exercising privacy rights or making privacy declarations restricted the processing that remains lawful.

TycoonX must not:

- create a “privacy withdrawal fee”;
- create a negative Diamond balance as compensation for withdrawing consent;
- demand the cash value of VIP because the player exercised a GDPR right;
- classify the exercise of a privacy right as chargeback abuse; or
- reduce future offers as retaliation for exercising privacy rights.

Separate fraud, exploit, or payment claims remain possible where supported by independent evidence, but the privacy-right exercise itself is not the misconduct.

## 7. BGB § 327p and Directive Article 16: user-created content after termination

Contract termination creates a second issue beyond GDPR personal-data deletion.

Under BGB § 327p and Article 16 of Directive (EU) 2019/770, the trader can have duties concerning **content other than personal data** that the consumer provided or created while using the digital product.

This is not the same as GDPR data portability.

### General non-use rule

After covered contract termination, CK-Labs must not continue using qualifying non-personal content the consumer provided or created, except where a statutory exception applies.

The German exceptions include content that:

1. has no utility outside the context of the supplied digital product;
2. relates only to the consumer's activity when using the supplied digital product;
3. has been aggregated with other data and cannot be disaggregated, or only with disproportionate effort; or
4. was generated jointly by the consumer and others and the other consumers can continue to use it.

Do not interpret these exceptions more broadly than the law supports.

## 8. Retrieval right for qualifying non-personal content

Under BGB § 327p(3), at the consumer's request CK-Labs must provide qualifying content covered by § 327p(2) sentence 1, subject to the statutory exceptions referenced there.

Where the retrieval right applies, the content must be provided:

- free of charge;
- without obstruction by CK-Labs;
- within a reasonable time; and
- in a commonly used, machine-readable format.

The German retrieval exclusion refers to the statutory categories corresponding to § 327p(2) sentence 2 numbers 1 to 3. Do not mechanically assume that jointly generated content in number 4 can never need to be provided to the requesting consumer.

### TycoonX examples requiring item-by-item classification

Potentially relevant content can include:

- original art uploaded or created by the player;
- original music or beats created through a TycoonX creative feature;
- original books or written works created by the player;
- profile or company content that is not itself personal data;
- collaborative content created with other players; and
- exported creative files or structured content where the content has utility outside TycoonX.

Other game data may fit a statutory exception, for example some state that has no utility outside TycoonX or only records the consumer's in-game activity. Do not make that assumption globally. Classify the content category.

Personal data within the same export remains governed by GDPR and other applicable data-protection law. A creative file can contain both non-personal content and personal-data metadata, so exports may need separation or redaction rather than an all-or-nothing rule.

## 9. Account deletion must not destroy a live retrieval request

The public TycoonX account-deletion flow currently explains that account-linked gameplay and social data may be removed. Add the following implementation safeguard:

- if a consumer makes a valid request to retrieve qualifying non-personal content under applicable digital-product law, preserve the content needed to answer that request until the request is completed or lawfully refused;
- do not complete destructive deletion in a way that makes a valid pending retrieval request impossible merely because the user also asked to close the account;
- where practical, let the player request the export before deletion, but do not state that pre-deletion timing is the only legally valid route if applicable law allows a post-termination request;
- after termination, CK-Labs may disable access to the service subject to the statutory retrieval right and other mandatory rights.

This does not require keeping an active playable account solely to fulfill an export. It requires preserving only what is needed to satisfy the applicable request and other lawful retention obligations.

## 10. Apple and Google account deletion remain separate platform obligations

Apple's current account-deletion guidance requires apps supporting account creation to let users initiate account deletion in-app and expects associated user-generated content to be deleted, subject to legal retention requirements.

Google Play separately requires qualifying apps that allow account creation to provide account-deletion mechanisms and to disclose legitimate retention where applicable.

Those platform deletion requirements do not erase a mandatory German/EU retrieval right that applies to qualifying non-personal content. The safe implementation sequence is:

1. identify the deletion request;
2. identify any pending statutory/contractual content-retrieval request;
3. preserve only the minimum content needed for that request and other lawful retention;
4. delete the active account and other deletable data; and
5. complete the export and then remove the temporary retained copy when no longer needed.

Do not keep broad UGC indefinitely under the label “export evidence.”

## 11. Apple, Google Play, and Xsolla payment records stay transaction-specific

The free-service/data analysis must not contaminate paid transaction logic.

- Apple purchase records remain Apple transaction evidence.
- Google Play order/purchase-token records remain Google transaction evidence.
- Xsolla transaction records remain Xsolla transaction evidence under the applicable merchant setup.
- A privacy-right exercise does not create a provider refund.
- A § 327p content retrieval request does not reverse a payment.
- A free-service conformity issue does not automatically revoke a valid paid entitlement.

Where narrow payment/entitlement records must be retained after account closure for tax, fraud, disputes, or legitimate restoration, use an appropriate legal basis and keep them separate from ordinary deleted profile/gameplay data as far as reasonably practicable.

## 12. Paid entitlement isolation

This gate must never cause:

- purchased Diamonds to be replayed, duplicated, or removed merely because the user withdrew optional consent;
- one-time 30-Day VIP to restart or shorten because a privacy request was submitted;
- limited-window Lifetime VIP to be revoked, duplicated, or converted merely because the user requested account deletion or content retrieval;
- a free promotional grant to be misrepresented as a paid provider transaction;
- an Apple, Google Play, or Xsolla refund to be initiated without the applicable transaction-specific basis; or
- an account-compromise investigation to be treated as consent withdrawal.

If account closure separately makes continued access impossible, apply the existing account-deletion, entitlement-restoration, consumer-remedy, and platform rules instead of inventing a new payment consequence.

## 13. Hacks, exploits, abuse, and moderation remain separate

A valid privacy or content-retrieval request is not evidence of:

- hacking;
- cheating;
- exploit use;
- promotion abuse;
- regional-price abuse;
- entitlement abuse;
- chargeback abuse; or
- malicious reporting.

CK-Labs can still investigate actual misconduct supported by separate evidence. A lawful exploit rollback can remove exploit-generated value without deleting unrelated purchased Diamonds or refusing a valid content request solely because the account is under investigation.

If a security emergency requires temporary access restriction, preserve legally required request deadlines and evidence as far as possible and explain any lawful delay where required.

## 14. Permanent service shutdown and successor scenarios

If TycoonX is permanently discontinued, sold, merged, reorganized, or transferred to a successor operator:

- apply the existing TycoonX shutdown/successor legal gates to paid entitlements and mandatory notices;
- identify whether users have a live right to retrieve qualifying non-personal content before or after contract termination;
- where feasible and legally required, provide a reasonable export opportunity rather than deleting exportable creative content without a path to retrieve it;
- transfer personal data only under an appropriate GDPR basis and notices; and
- do not use a business transfer to expand the use of terminated users' non-personal content beyond what the contract and law permit.

A successor operator may continue the service and valid entitlements where the applicable transaction and law permit it, but the transfer does not erase non-waivable consumer or privacy rights.

## 15. Release test matrix

Before considering this area production-ready, test at least:

1. free account using only data necessary for authentication/service delivery;
2. free account with optional analytics consent refused from the beginning;
3. active player withdraws optional analytics consent;
4. player objects to an eligible legitimate-interest processing activity;
5. privacy withdrawal where the affected processing is genuinely necessary for one optional feature but not the whole account;
6. account deletion with no user-created exportable content;
7. account deletion after the player requests an export of an original art file;
8. post-termination request for an original music/book file that remains available in retained records;
9. collaborative company/community content requiring joint-content analysis;
10. content that has no utility outside TycoonX and is documented as a § 327p exception;
11. export containing both creative content and personal-data metadata;
12. user with purchased Diamonds who withdraws optional consent;
13. user with active 30-Day VIP who submits a GDPR request;
14. user with valid Lifetime VIP who deletes the account and separately requests content retrieval;
15. exploit investigation occurring at the same time as a valid privacy/content request; and
16. permanent shutdown with outstanding qualifying content-retrieval requests.

For each scenario, preserve the legal classification, actual processing purpose, user notice/request, action taken, affected content categories, entitlement impact, and final outcome.

## 16. Public-page rule

The TycoonX account-deletion page should not promise that every bit of user-created content is always immediately and irreversibly destroyed if applicable law requires temporary preservation or gives the consumer a separate right to retrieve qualifying content.

The page should instead state, in clear player-facing language, that deletion does not waive any mandatory right to retrieve qualifying content created or provided by the user and that TycoonX Support can handle such a request where applicable.

This is not an invitation to retain unnecessary data. GDPR minimization and deletion duties still apply to personal data, while BGB § 327p governs the separate non-personal-content question where its conditions are met.

## 17. Regression rules

Do not weaken this gate to say that:

- “free” always means §§ 327 et seq. BGB cannot apply;
- personal data is money or ordinary payment consideration;
- every use of personal data automatically means § 327(3) applies despite the § 312(1a) exception;
- withdrawing consent automatically terminates the TycoonX contract;
- exercising a GDPR right permits CK-Labs to claim damages from the player merely because processing became more limited;
- deleting an account automatically waives a qualifying content-retrieval right;
- GDPR portability and BGB § 327p are the same right;
- all in-game state must always be exportable under § 327p;
- collaborative content can always be withheld from the requesting user merely because others also created it;
- account deletion creates a refund; or
- privacy/content requests justify taking unrelated purchased Diamonds, 30-Day VIP, or Lifetime VIP.

## Founder-protective interpretation

The purpose of this gate is not to promise unlimited free-service support, indefinite storage, blanket export of all server state, or a refund for a free product. It gives CK-Labs a defensible classification model: identify when mandatory digital-product law actually applies, keep GDPR and contract law separate, preserve only the content that must lawfully be retrievable, use statutory exceptions where they genuinely fit, and prevent privacy/deletion workflows from corrupting paid entitlement records.
