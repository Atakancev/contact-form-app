# TycoonX UGC Copyright & UrhDaG Release Gate

Last reviewed: 2026-09-02  
Operator/business name used in player-facing documents: **CK-Labs**

This is an operational release gate for copyright, creator-content, and intellectual-property handling in TycoonX. It supplements the canonical TycoonX Terms of Service, Community Standards & Moderation Policy, the DSA moderation gates, the German digital-service/creator-content exit gate, and applicable Apple/Google platform rules.

TycoonX went to full release on **September 1, 2026**. The live game, users, Diamonds, one-time 30-Day VIP, Lifetime VIP, rewards, and purchases must not be described as beta.

## 1. P0: classify TycoonX before assuming the German UrhDaG applies

TycoonX supports or may support user-created art, music, beats, books, text, images, profile content, company/union content, chat, and other UGC. That alone does **not** automatically make TycoonX an online content-sharing service provider under the German Urheberrechts-Diensteanbieter-Gesetz (UrhDaG) or Article 17 of Directive (EU) 2019/790.

Before launching or materially expanding public creator-content functionality, keep a dated classification memo addressing the actual service facts.

Under current § 2(1) UrhDaG, the relevant service category requires, cumulatively, that the service:

1. has as a main purpose, exclusively or among its main purposes, storing and making publicly accessible a **large amount** of copyright-protected content uploaded by third parties;
2. organises that content;
3. promotes that content for profit; and
4. competes with online content services for the same audiences.

The EU Article 17 framework is likewise aimed at services whose main or one of main purposes is storing and giving the public access to a large amount of user-uploaded protected content which the service organises and promotes for profit.

Do not classify TycoonX merely from the presence of upload buttons. Record at least:

- the main commercial purpose of TycoonX;
- how prominent public art/music/book/creator discovery is compared with the core business-simulation game;
- total creator-content volume and active audience;
- whether CK-Labs actively organises, recommends, ranks, categorises, curates, promotes, or monetises access to that content;
- whether the creator-content surface competes for audiences with music, image, book, video, or other online content services;
- whether public content is incidental to gameplay/community use or has become one of the Service's main purposes; and
- any planned change that would turn the creator gallery into a materially larger content-distribution product.

**Current release assumption:** the repository does not declare TycoonX to be an UrhDaG service provider. Production facts must support that position. If public creator-content becomes a main purpose or materially approaches the statutory criteria, obtain a fresh classification before rollout rather than relying on this assumption.

## 2. Do not confuse ordinary hosting/DSA duties with UrhDaG Article 17 duties

Even if TycoonX is **not** an UrhDaG service provider, CK-Labs can still have obligations when specific UGC allegedly infringes copyright, trademark, publicity, privacy, or other third-party rights.

The existing TycoonX Community Standards already:

- require users to have rights necessary for submitted content;
- prohibit intellectual-property infringement;
- provide an intellectual-property complaint route;
- permit proportionate removal/restriction after review; and
- preserve DSA notice-and-action, statement-of-reasons, complaint, and moderation safeguards where applicable.

Do not import a US-style “DMCA safe harbour” label into EU/German legal copy unless a US-specific workflow genuinely requires it. A notice can still contain similar practical information, but German/EU liability and redress rules must be analysed under their own legal framework.

## 3. Current user-content license is intentionally limited

The canonical TycoonX Terms and Community Standards currently say users retain the rights they hold in UGC and grant CK-Labs a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free operational license.

Keep the license founder-protective but proportionate:

- host, store, reproduce, format, translate, transmit, display, moderate, back up, and technically adapt UGC as reasonably necessary to operate, secure, moderate, support, and improve TycoonX;
- display intentionally public UGC in the Service;
- feature public UGC for TycoonX community/promotional purposes only where consistent with context, settings, applicable law, and third-party rights;
- do not turn private messages, support communications, or non-public reports into promotional content merely because CK-Labs can technically access them;
- do not silently claim ownership of the user's copyright;
- do not silently convert the operational license into an unrestricted perpetual merchandising, sublicensing, resale, or AI-training right; and
- end the active-system license on deletion except where lawful continued processing is needed for backups, legal obligations, security, moderation evidence, disputes, or content that cannot reasonably be recalled after lawful sharing.

If CK-Labs later wants materially broader commercial reuse of creator works, update the canonical Terms/Community Policy first and then synchronize the affected document type across all 25 locales before relying on that broader right.

## 4. Rights provenance before CK-Labs promotes creator content

A user saying “I uploaded it” is not proof that they own every right in it.

Before CK-Labs manually features, republishes, advertises, sells, licenses, or otherwise commercially highlights a creator work outside ordinary in-Service display, consider the rights actually implicated, including where relevant:

- copyright in the underlying work;
- rights in music composition and lyrics;
- rights in sound recordings and performances;
- photographs, artwork, book text, fonts, stock assets, samples, loops, and templates;
- trademark or brand use;
- personality/publicity/image rights;
- co-authors or joint creators;
- AI-generated or AI-assisted material and the provenance of inputs where relevant;
- third-party platform/service terms; and
- territorial or duration restrictions on a license.

For a curated TycoonX promotion, retain reasonable evidence of permission or provenance proportionate to the use. Do not assume that a user's general upload license automatically authorises every external ad campaign or commercial exploitation scenario.

## 5. Intellectual-property complaint intake

TycoonX Support or another designated route must remain capable of receiving IP complaints without requiring a rights holder to guess an internal database ID.

A useful complaint should request, where reasonably available:

- the claimant's identity and contact route;
- identification of the right or protected work;
- enough information to locate the challenged TycoonX content, such as URL, content ID, creator profile, title, screenshot, or other unique locator;
- why the claimant believes the use is unauthorised or otherwise unlawful;
- the claimant's relationship to the right, such as author, owner, licensee, representative, or agent;
- any relevant evidence of ownership/authority; and
- a good-faith statement sufficient for the applicable process.

Do not reject an otherwise actionable complaint merely because it does not use a CK-Labs template. Do not require excessive identity documents where simpler evidence is sufficient.

## 6. Triage: report is evidence, not automatic proof

An IP complaint does not automatically prove infringement.

For each report, assess as appropriate:

- whether the identified content exists and is accessible;
- whether the claimant appears connected to the asserted right;
- whether the report identifies the allegedly protected material with reasonable precision;
- whether the challenged use might be licensed, public-domain, subject to quotation, parody, pastiche, another statutory exception, or otherwise lawful;
- whether immediate temporary restriction is reasonably necessary to prevent serious ongoing harm;
- whether the uploader should be invited to provide license/permission evidence or context;
- whether DSA notice-and-action and statement-of-reasons rules apply; and
- whether evidence must be preserved for a dispute, authority request, fraud investigation, or court process.

CK-Labs may temporarily restrict a specific work while a credible complaint is investigated where proportionate. Do not automatically suspend the entire account for a first disputed copyright claim unless the circumstances independently justify it.

## 7. Repeat and serious infringement

Subject to applicable law, evidence, proportionality, DSA safeguards, and the Terms, CK-Labs may escalate against users who repeatedly or seriously upload infringing material.

Possible measures include:

- warning;
- removal/restriction of the specific content;
- temporary upload restrictions;
- loss of creator/community publishing privileges;
- temporary account restriction where necessary; and
- account termination for serious or repeated violations where lawful.

A copyright restriction must not be used as a pretext to confiscate unrelated legitimate paid digital value. Separate entitlement/refund/termination rules continue to apply to Diamonds, one-time 30-Day VIP, and Lifetime VIP.

## 8. False, abusive, or bad-faith copyright complaints

Do not assume every claimant is genuine.

Maintain safeguards for:

- impersonated rights holders;
- forged authorization letters;
- attempts to remove a competitor's original work;
- claims over public-domain material;
- repeated notices aimed at criticism, parody, or other lawful uses;
- automated notices that identify the wrong work; and
- attempts to use copyright complaints to obtain private user information.

Where the UrhDaG applies, § 18 contains specific anti-abuse consequences for repeated or negligent false blocking demands. Even where that statute does not apply, TycoonX may reject demonstrably abusive notices, preserve evidence, rate-limit a malicious reporting account, or require stronger verification before further high-impact action where lawful.

Do not disclose uploader identity, private messages, payment details, IP addresses, or other personal data to a claimant merely because they sent a copyright complaint. Any disclosure must have its own lawful basis or binding legal process.

## 9. If TycoonX becomes an UrhDaG service provider: Article 17-style obligations become a P0 legal release gate

If the production classification concludes that § 2 UrhDaG applies, do **not** launch or continue the materially changed creator-content surface on the ordinary-hosting checklist alone.

At minimum, reopen and implement the current UrhDaG duties applicable to the actual service, including:

### 9.1 Licensing efforts

Under § 4 UrhDaG an in-scope provider must make best efforts to acquire contractual rights for public communication of protected works in the circumstances specified by the statute, including rights offered on appropriate terms and relevant repertoires available through known representative rightholders or German collecting societies where the statutory conditions are met.

Do not promise that the small size of CK-Labs automatically removes this duty.

### 9.2 Simple and qualified blocking

Under § 8, a sufficiently substantiated rightholder notice can trigger an obligation to end an unauthorised public communication. Future blocking can require the additional information specified by law.

Under § 7, qualified blocking obligations apply subject to proportionality and the statutory provider-category rules. Importantly:

- startup provider status is not the same thing as “indie developer”;
- § 2(2) currently defines a startup service provider by EU annual turnover up to **€10 million** and availability to the EU public for **less than three years**;
- the startup exemption in § 7(4) for qualified blocking applies while average monthly unique visitors to the service's websites do not exceed **5 million**; and
- § 2(3) defines a small service provider by EU annual turnover up to **€1 million**, for which § 7(5) contains a rebuttable proportionality presumption concerning qualified blocking.

Keep dated turnover, service-age, and audience evidence if relying on those provisions. Do not extrapolate them to unrelated UrhDaG duties.

### 9.3 Lawful uses and overblocking protection

If automated blocking is used, implement the current §§ 9-11 safeguards rather than assuming a match always means infringement.

Current UrhDaG rules protect or create procedures around lawful quotation, caricature, parody, pastiche, and other statutory permitted uses. They also contain rules for presumed permitted uses and user marking in applicable circumstances.

Do not build a copyright filter whose operational rule is simply “hash match = permanent ban.”

### 9.4 Terms must specifically mention statutory permitted uses

If TycoonX becomes an UrhDaG service provider, § 5(3) requires the provider to inform users in its terms about the statutory permitted uses covered by § 5(1).

The current generic statement that mandatory rights remain unaffected is **not a substitute for this specific in-scope duty**.

Therefore, an affirmative UrhDaG classification is a **canonical-Terms localization trigger**:

1. update the English TycoonX Terms first;
2. update the rendered English Terms page;
3. reopen the Terms document in all 25 locales in the required order; and
4. update `TYCOONX_LEGAL_LOCALIZATION_PROGRESS.md` after each localization change.

Do not patch only German text.

### 9.5 Internal copyright complaint procedure

If § 14 UrhDaG applies, provide an effective, free, and rapid complaint procedure for users and rightholders concerning blocking/public communication. Current § 14 requires notice to all participants, opportunity to comment, a decision no later than one week after the complaint, and an impartial human decision-maker.

The general TycoonX Support appeal route is not automatically sufficient unless it actually meets those statutory conditions.

## 10. Current UrhDaG statutory exceptions are not universal copyright permissions

Do not tell players that “15 seconds of music is always legal,” “160 characters are always free,” or “small images can always be copied.”

Current § 10 UrhDaG contains quantitative thresholds for a particular presumed-permitted-use mechanism when the UrhDaG applies and the statutory conditions are met. Those thresholds are **not** a general license for copying third-party works anywhere in TycoonX or outside the UrhDaG framework.

Likewise, parody, quotation, or pastiche depends on the applicable legal conditions. The existence of an exception must be assessed in context.

## 11. Joint creations, company/union content, and ownership disputes

TycoonX can involve collaborative companies, unions, art, music, books, comments, or other shared projects.

Do not assume the account that presses “publish” automatically owns every underlying right. If multiple users jointly create a work, ownership/use may depend on applicable co-authorship rules and their actual contributions/agreements.

Operational safeguards:

- avoid transferring one user's creator work to another user merely because a company, union, or account owner changed;
- keep creator attribution/provenance where operationally reasonable;
- when a collaboration dispute arises, freeze only the disputed publication/use if necessary rather than unrelated game assets;
- do not adjudicate complex copyright ownership beyond what is necessary to operate the Service unless reliable evidence supports the decision; and
- direct parties to appropriate legal resolution where CK-Labs cannot safely determine ownership.

## 12. Account deletion and BGB § 327p interaction

The separate `TYCOONX_GERMAN_DATA_FOR_DIGITAL_SERVICE_UGC_EXIT_RELEASE_GATE.md` controls qualifying non-personal creator-content return after contract termination under German digital-product law.

Copyright moderation and account deletion are separate questions.

Before destructive deletion:

- identify creator works that may need to be returned/exported;
- preserve only what is lawfully necessary for an active infringement dispute, legal claim, moderation evidence, or other statutory purpose;
- do not keep public use of deleted creator content merely because an old database copy still exists; and
- do not let an infringement complaint erase an independent statutory content-return right where that right still applies.

## 13. AI and creator content

Do not use TycoonX creator content for external model training, dataset sale, or unrelated generative-AI product development merely because the operational UGC license says CK-Labs may “improve TycoonX.”

Any materially different AI-training use requires a separate rights, privacy, transparency, platform-policy, and contract analysis before launch. Where consent or a broader license is required, obtain it rather than stretching the current limited license.

AI-generated content uploaded by players remains subject to the same IP, impersonation, safety, and platform rules. A user selecting an AI tool does not guarantee that the output is free of third-party rights.

## 14. Apple App Store parity

Current Apple App Review Guidelines require apps to have rights to the content they use and specifically identify intellectual-property infringement as a UGC risk. Apple also requires UGC apps to provide filtering/moderation, reporting, blocking, and published contact information.

Before an iOS release affecting creator content:

- [ ] verify CK-Labs owns or licenses bundled/store-listing art, music, screenshots, fonts, brands, and other protected material;
- [ ] keep UGC reporting/blocking/moderation functioning;
- [ ] avoid enabling unauthorised downloading/conversion of third-party media sources;
- [ ] retain permission evidence for third-party material Apple may ask about; and
- [ ] ensure creator-content monetisation, if introduced, also satisfies Apple's applicable payment rules.

Apple approval does not prove German/EU copyright compliance and does not determine UrhDaG classification.

## 15. Google Play parity

Current Google Play policy prohibits apps/developer accounts from infringing or encouraging infringement of copyright, trademark, patent, trade secret, and other proprietary rights. Google also applies its UGC policy to user-contributed content available to other users.

Before an Android release affecting creator content:

- [ ] verify bundled/store-listing content rights;
- [ ] keep robust, ongoing UGC moderation;
- [ ] require applicable Terms/User Policy acceptance before users create/upload UGC where Play policy requires it;
- [ ] do not market TycoonX as a way to download, copy, stream, or redistribute third-party works without authorization;
- [ ] keep evidence for licensed third-party logos, imagery, music, or other assets; and
- [ ] re-check policy after material creator-feature changes.

Google's own copyright notice process for Google Play does not replace CK-Labs' handling of infringement inside TycoonX.

## 16. Store/payment/provider separation

Apple, Google, and Xsolla can control payment, storefront, tax, fraud, or platform-review functions. They do not automatically become the copyright licensor for user works hosted by TycoonX.

Similarly:

- an Apple/Google/Xsolla transaction record does not prove ownership of an uploaded work;
- a copyright claim does not prove that a payment was fraudulent;
- a payment refund does not decide a copyright dispute;
- a chargeback does not transfer copyright;
- Xsolla merchant-of-record status does not itself give CK-Labs rights to creator works; and
- a provider outage does not authorize permanent republication of content that CK-Labs no longer has a right to use.

## 17. Paid entitlement isolation

Copyright enforcement must be scoped to the affected content/account conduct.

A copyright complaint, takedown, appeal, creator-export request, or UrhDaG classification change must not by itself:

- delete or duplicate legitimately purchased Diamonds;
- create a negative Diamond balance unrelated to the disputed content;
- restart, extend, pause, or duplicate the original one-time 30-Day VIP period;
- insert an expiry into valid Lifetime VIP;
- convert Lifetime VIP into 30-Day VIP;
- replay an Apple, Google Play, or Xsolla purchase event;
- manufacture a refund, reversal, or chargeback; or
- label the user a hacker/exploiter without separate evidence.

If an account is lawfully terminated for serious/repeated infringement, paid entitlements remain governed by the canonical Terms, mandatory consumer law, platform restoration rules, and transaction-specific facts. No copyright clause waives non-waivable remedies.

## 19. Regression scenarios

Test at least the following scenarios after a material creator-content change:

1. Player uploads original artwork and later deletes it.
2. Player uploads an image copied from another artist without permission.
3. Rights holder submits a precise complaint with a valid content locator.
4. Third party submits a vague complaint with no identifiable work.
5. Fraudster impersonates a known artist and requests takedown of the artist's own work.
6. Player supplies a plausible license after a temporary restriction.
7. User uploads a parody/pastiche/quotation that may be legally permitted.
8. Automated matching incorrectly flags public-domain material.
9. CK-Labs wants to feature a player's artwork in an external TycoonX ad.
10. A music upload contains a licensed beat but an unlicensed vocal sample.
11. Two users dispute ownership of a jointly created TycoonX book/music project.
12. Creator deletes account while an active infringement dispute exists.
13. German user requests return of qualifying non-personal creator content after termination.
14. Public creator galleries grow sharply and become a major discovery surface.
15. TycoonX begins ranking/promoting user music for profit against music-content services.
16. Service qualifies as UrhDaG startup provider below 5 million monthly unique visitors.
17. Service exceeds the startup visitor threshold.
18. A small-provider turnover threshold is exceeded in a later year.
19. Player repeatedly uploads works after confirmed infringement notices.
20. Claimant repeatedly sends false blocking demands.
21. Apple asks for proof of rights to a third-party image used in the App Store listing.
22. Google Play flags apparently copyrighted creator content visible in the app.
23. Copyright complaint arrives while the player's Xsolla refund is independently pending.
24. Copyright restriction applies while the player owns valid Lifetime VIP.
25. Copyright complaint involves a Diamond-purchased creator feature but not the Diamond purchase itself.
26. AI-assisted art includes a recognizable third-party character or logo.
27. CK-Labs wants to use creator content for external AI-model training.
28. Provider migration moves stored creator files but does not change the UGC license scope.

- [ ] CK-Labs externally promotes user works without a reasonable rights/provenance basis;

- [ ] creator deletion/export behavior conflicts with the applicable German digital-product/content-return rules.
