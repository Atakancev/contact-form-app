# TycoonX Legal Document Change, Notice & Versioning Release Gate

**Status:** P0 legal / consumer / privacy / moderation release gate  
**Owner:** CK-Labs  
**Last reviewed:** September 1, 2026  
**Scope:** TycoonX Terms of Service, Purchases & Refunds Policy, Privacy Policy, Community Standards, localized legal pages, checkout/support copy that incorporates those documents, and any future material amendment presented to existing users.

## Purpose

TycoonX legal documents can and will need to evolve as laws, platform rules, payment providers, security requirements, features, game mechanics and infrastructure change. The founder-protective objective is to make those updates efficiently without creating an unfair unilateral-amendment process, hiding material changes, applying a new rule retroactively, or treating a Privacy Policy notice as consent when consent is legally required.

The canonical English TycoonX documents remain authoritative for drafting and localization. This gate is an operational control. It does not itself amend the player-facing contract and does not waive mandatory consumer, privacy, digital-product or platform rights.

## 1. Classify every legal-document edit before publication

Every edit must be classified as one of these categories before it reaches production:

1. **Non-material editorial correction**: spelling, grammar, formatting, broken link, clearer wording that does not change legal meaning, contact-detail correction, or another purely administrative fix.
2. **Material contractual change**: changes rights, obligations, account restrictions, liability, dispute procedures, entitlement treatment, pricing logic, purchase/refund consequences, suspension/termination rules, or another contractual consequence for existing users.
3. **Digital-product/service modification**: the document change accompanies or describes a real change to an ongoing digital product or service, such as material VIP functionality, access, compatibility, economy design or a supported-provider migration. Route this separately through the existing BGB § 327r / Directive (EU) 2019/770 change gate.
4. **Privacy-processing change**: a new purpose, legal basis, recipient/category, international-transfer arrangement, retention rule, tracking technology, profiling activity, automated decision or other material processing change. Route this separately through the GDPR and TDDDG gates.
5. **Community/moderation change**: changes what user content is allowed, how restrictions are imposed, moderation tools/procedures, appeals or account/content enforcement. Assess DSA Article 14 and the other applicable DSA gates.
6. **Purchase/payment-policy change**: changes future checkout, refunds, withdrawal handling, chargeback treatment, entitlement delivery, regional pricing, promotions, taxes or payment-provider allocation. Assess the relevant Apple, Google Play, Xsolla, EU/German checkout and consumer-law gates.

A single edit can fall into more than one category. Classification is about legal effect, not the filename containing the text.

## 2. Non-material edits do not justify unnecessary re-consent

Do not force every existing player through an acceptance wall merely because:

- a typo was corrected;
- a dead URL was replaced;
- formatting improved;
- a legal citation was updated without changing the player's rights;
- a support address or equivalent administrative detail changed; or
- a sentence was clarified without changing its legal meaning.

For these edits, keep ordinary version history and update localizations where necessary for consistency, but do not create consent fatigue or imply that CK-Labs needs a fresh contract every time punctuation changes.

If an edit described as "clarification" actually expands CK-Labs' rights or reduces the user's rights, classify it by substance as material.

## 3. Material Terms changes require a documented legal basis and clear explanation

For a material contractual amendment affecting existing users, record before publication:

- the old clause and new clause;
- the reason for the change;
- the affected user/product population;
- whether the existing contract validly permits the type of change;
- whether notice alone is legally sufficient or affirmative agreement is needed;
- whether the user has a rejection/termination route;
- the notice channel and timing;
- the effective date; and
- any mandatory remedy caused by an associated digital-product change.

The canonical Terms already state that CK-Labs may update them for valid reasons such as changes in law, platform requirements, payment providers, security, technical operation and new features, while preserving legally required notice and consent. That clause is a starting point, not a blank cheque.

German BGB § 307 remains relevant to standard terms: a clause can be ineffective where it unreasonably disadvantages the consumer contrary to good faith, including through lack of clarity or comprehensibility. Do not write or operationalize an amendment clause as "CK-Labs may change anything at any time for any reason and continued use always means acceptance."

## 4. No retroactive rewrite of completed transactions or historical conduct

A later legal-document version must not silently rewrite the legal consequences of an earlier completed one-time transaction or past conduct.

Examples:

- A later Diamond price increase does not create an extra charge for a completed earlier purchase.
- A later lower price does not automatically create a refund or price-match right for an earlier purchase unless mandatory law, provider rules or the original offer says otherwise.
- A later refund-policy version does not retroactively extinguish a statutory withdrawal or conformity right that already applied.
- A later Lifetime VIP description cannot transform a valid Lifetime VIP purchase into a 30-Day VIP entitlement.
- A later exploit rule cannot turn ordinary historical gameplay that was permitted at the time into intentional abuse solely because the rule changed later.

Security/economy corrections for actual invalid state, exploits, duplicated grants, reversed payments or fraud remain possible under the existing Terms and mandatory-law limits. This rule prevents retroactive rewriting, not legitimate correction based on the rules and facts that lawfully apply.

## 5. Notice is not automatically consent

A banner saying "our Terms changed" proves only that a notice may have been shown. It does not automatically prove that the user accepted a new contractual term where affirmative agreement is legally required.

Likewise:

- opening TycoonX after an update is not automatically valid consent for every possible material contractual amendment;
- continuing gameplay is not automatically GDPR consent;
- a Privacy Policy update is not itself a GDPR legal basis;
- a support email is not automatically a valid waiver of statutory rights; and
- accepting one specific new feature does not automatically accept unrelated commercial terms unless the process clearly and lawfully links them.

Where the applicable legal framework permits notice/continued use for the particular change, preserve evidence of the legal basis and the notice. Where express consent or agreement is required, capture that affirmative action separately enough to prove it.

## 6. Consumer-facing change summaries must explain the real effect

For a material change, the user-facing notice should identify the practical effect rather than merely stating "we updated our policies."

A useful notice normally answers:

- What changed?
- Why did it change?
- When does it take effect?
- Which TycoonX users/products does it affect?
- Does it change a user's rights or obligations?
- Does the user need to take any action?
- Can the user reject, terminate or use another remedy where applicable?
- Where can the full old and new documents be reviewed?

Avoid dense change summaries that hide the important point in generic legal language.

The European Commission's CPC enforcement work concerning WhatsApp is a useful enforcement-risk signal: WhatsApp committed to explain future changes and their effect on rights/obligations, make rejection as prominent as acceptance, allow update notices to be dismissed/reviewed later, respect user choices, and refrain from recurring pressure notifications. Those WhatsApp-specific commitments are **not** automatically a standalone legal rule binding every TycoonX amendment, but they are a strong warning against coercive or opaque update UX.

## 7. Rejection must be handled lawfully, not punitively

Where a user has a legal or contractual right to reject a proposed change, do not fake a choice by making "Reject" merely perform the same action as "Accept."

A rejection can have consequences where the service genuinely cannot continue under the old terms and applicable law permits termination or another change. In that case:

- explain the consequence before the user chooses;
- distinguish account/service termination from forfeiture of unrelated paid value;
- preserve mandatory notice, digital-product, refund, price-reduction, conformity and data rights;
- do not characterize the user's rejection as fraud, abuse or chargeback misconduct; and
- do not repeatedly nag a user into accepting after a legally effective choice simply because acceptance would be commercially convenient.

If applicable law requires a different route, follow that route rather than this general workflow.

## 8. Privacy Policy changes need a separate GDPR analysis

The Privacy Policy is a transparency document, not a universal consent form.

Before adding a new processing activity, determine:

- the specific purpose;
- categories of personal data;
- legal basis under GDPR Article 6 and, where relevant, Article 9;
- recipients/processors;
- international-transfer mechanism;
- retention rule;
- whether TDDDG consent is separately required for device access/storage;
- whether a DPIA is required; and
- whether the existing Privacy Policy accurately explains the processing.

Under GDPR Article 13(3), where personal data collected from the data subject will be further processed for a different purpose, the controller must provide information about that other purpose and relevant Article 13(2) information **before** the further processing, subject to the Regulation's rules and exceptions. Article 14(4) contains the corresponding rule for data not obtained from the data subject.

Updating the Privacy Policy after the new processing has already started is not a substitute for prior information where prior information is required.

If the intended processing needs consent, obtain valid consent. If another lawful basis genuinely applies, do not manufacture a consent checkbox merely for appearance.

## 9. Community Standards and moderation changes need a DSA scope check

Where TycoonX functionality falls within the DSA intermediary-service framework, Article 14 requires terms to clearly explain applicable restrictions on user-provided information, including relevant moderation policies/procedures/tools, algorithmic decision-making/human review and complaint procedures. Article 14(2) requires recipients to be informed of significant changes to the terms and conditions.

The DSA recital gives examples such as changes to rules on information permitted on the service or other changes that directly affect recipients' ability to use the service.

Operational rule:

- do not silently deploy a significant new content/account restriction while leaving the published Community Standards materially stale;
- keep the moderation policy that was actually in effect for an enforcement decision identifiable;
- distinguish a later policy version from the policy that applied when older content was posted; and
- route appeals and Article 17 reason statements through the existing DSA gates where applicable.

Do not assume that every TycoonX feature has identical DSA classification. Keep the feature-by-feature scope analysis.

## 10. Purchases & Refunds changes cannot rewrite provider records

A new policy version must not change the authoritative historical transaction facts held by Apple, Google Play, Xsolla and the TycoonX entitlement ledger.

For every material Purchases & Refunds update:

- preserve the old version associated with already completed transactions where relevant;
- preserve provider transaction/order identifiers and transaction-time price/currency/SKU evidence;
- preserve the one-time nature and original expiry calculation of 30-Day VIP;
- preserve Lifetime VIP's original valid purchase status and sales-window representation;
- preserve purchased versus promotional/gameplay Diamond provenance where relevant;
- keep failed, pending, reversed, refunded and chargeback states transaction-specific; and
- do not use a policy change to claw back unrelated legitimate purchases.

Apple, Google Play and Xsolla may change their own rules. A provider-rule change can justify updating TycoonX terms and flows prospectively, but it does not automatically rewrite a completed transaction or waive CK-Labs' own non-transferable consumer obligations.

## 11. Price, tax, currency and promotion changes are prospective unless law says otherwise

For future purchases CK-Labs may change Diamond bundle prices/content, VIP prices, regional prices, currencies and genuine promotions, subject to law and provider rules.

The legal-document release process must preserve these existing principles:

- prices may differ by platform, country and channel;
- VAT/tax and provider FX adjustments may change local prices;
- the final total price shown before confirmation governs the completed transaction, subject to lawful obvious-error correction;
- completed one-time purchases are not silently repriced later;
- a later lower price does not automatically create a credit/refund/price match;
- a later higher price does not create a new charge for the earlier purchase;
- Lifetime VIP may have different genuine prices in different selected sales windows;
- promotional countdown, scarcity and comparison-price claims must remain truthful; and
- any future recurring product requires its own compliant recurring-price-change/notice rules.

## 12. Emergency legal/security changes still need post-change discipline

Sometimes a law, security incident, fraud attack or provider mandate requires urgent action before normal notice timing is practical.

Where immediate action is genuinely necessary:

1. record the legal/security/provider reason and timestamp;
2. limit the emergency change to what is reasonably necessary;
3. separate immediate technical containment from permanent contractual consequences;
4. notify affected users as soon as legally and reasonably appropriate;
5. provide required durable-medium notice or consent afterward if the applicable law requires it;
6. preserve mandatory remedies; and
7. replace temporary emergency language with a properly reviewed canonical version once the incident stabilizes.

Calling something an "emergency" does not create a general right to waive mandatory consumer or privacy rules.

## 13. Versioning and evidence are mandatory for material changes

For each material public legal version, retain a lightweight immutable change record containing at least:

- document name;
- version/effective date;
- previous version identifier;
- exact old/new text or a reproducible diff;
- plain-language change summary;
- reason/legal trigger;
- approving/reviewing person;
- affected countries/platforms/products;
- notice audience;
- notice copy/channel/send timestamp;
- acceptance/consent mechanism if required;
- rejection/termination route if applicable;
- localization status; and
- links/hashes identifying the published versions.

Do not retain unnecessary player data simply to prove a document changed. Aggregate delivery evidence where sufficient; retain account-specific acceptance records only where the legal/contractual need justifies them and the Privacy Policy/retention schedule supports it.

## 14. Old versions must remain retrievable internally

Do not overwrite the only copy of an old legal document.

CK-Labs should be able to answer questions such as:

- What Terms applied when this Lifetime VIP was purchased?
- What Purchases Policy was shown when this Diamond transaction occurred?
- What Community Standard was in force when this moderation decision was made?
- What Privacy Policy described the processing when this data was collected?

Internal archival access does not mean every historical version must remain the default public policy forever. It means disputes can be investigated from real evidence rather than reconstructing old terms from memory.

## 15. Localization synchronization is part of the release, not an afterthought

The English TycoonX legal documents remain canonical for drafting. When canonical legal meaning materially changes:

1. update the English canonical document first;
2. reopen only the affected localized document type in `TYCOONX_LEGAL_LOCALIZATION_PROGRESS.md`;
3. synchronize the 25 locales in the exact required order: `tr`, `de`, `es`, `es_MX`, `fr`, `fr_CA`, `it`, `pt`, `pt_BR`, `ru`, `ja`, `ko`, `zh`, `zh_Hans`, `zh_Hant`, `ar`, `nl`, `sv`, `nb`, `pl`, `th`, `vi`, `uk`, `hi`, `id`;
4. preserve genuinely localized variants rather than copying neighboring locales;
5. preserve RTL for Arabic;
6. update the progress tracker after each localization change; and
7. do not describe a localized version as current when it still expresses the superseded legal meaning.

A material English change may go live urgently where law/security truly requires it, but the localization backlog and interim disclosure must be explicit rather than silently presenting stale localized terms as current.

## 16. Paid-entitlement isolation during legal-document updates

Accepting, rejecting, viewing or being notified about a legal-document update must not by itself:

- grant, spend, replay or confiscate Diamonds;
- reset, restart, shorten or duplicate the one-time 30-Day VIP clock;
- revoke, duplicate or convert Lifetime VIP;
- initiate an Apple, Google Play or Xsolla refund;
- classify the account as chargeback, promotion, regional-pricing or entitlement abuse; or
- erase transaction evidence.

If a lawful contract consequence later affects service access, apply that consequence separately with the product-specific mandatory-rights analysis. Legal-document state and entitlement state must not share an unsafe catch-all flag.

## 17. Release scenarios to test

Before declaring production parity, retain evidence for at least these scenarios:

| Scenario | Expected control |
| --- | --- |
| Typo correction only | Version recorded; no forced re-consent; localization consistency checked |
| Material Terms restriction for existing users | Old/new diff, valid reason, notice/agreement analysis, clear effect summary, rejection/termination analysis |
| New analytics purpose | GDPR/TDDDG analysis completed before processing; Privacy Policy notice is not treated as consent |
| New Community Standards prohibition | DSA scope checked; significant-change notice where applicable; enforcement tied to correct policy version |
| Apple/Google/Xsolla rule change | Future-flow update separated from completed transaction rights and historical provider records |
| New Diamond refund wording | Purchased-Diamond withdrawal/conformity rules preserved; no retroactive waiver |
| New Lifetime VIP description | Existing valid Lifetime VIP not converted or shortened; future sales copy synchronized |
| Urgent fraud/security change | Emergency basis recorded; temporary containment separated from final contractual consequence; later notice/review completed |
| User rejects a material amendment where rejection is available | Choice respected; consequences explained; unrelated paid value not automatically forfeited |
| Localized document still has old meaning | Release blocked or interim mismatch explicitly handled; tracker reopened until synchronized |

## 18. Brand and release-status invariants

- In all displayed legal/player/support text, spell the game name exactly **TycoonX**.
- Technical routes/file names containing `tyconx` may remain where changing them could break URLs or integrations.
- TycoonX is in full release from **September 1, 2026**.
- Do not describe the live Service, current users, current legal terms, Diamonds, 30-Day VIP, Lifetime VIP or current rewards as beta.
- Genuine test, staging, review, free, promotional or complimentary grants may be described accurately where legally useful without implying that the live game is a beta.

## Release decision

Do not mark the legal-document change path production-ready until CK-Labs can prove that material updates are classified before publication, old versions remain reconstructable, users receive an intelligible description of material changes where required, Privacy Policy changes are not misused as consent, DSA-significant Terms changes are notified where applicable, completed transactions are not retroactively rewritten, paid entitlements remain isolated, and every material canonical change is synchronized through the localization tracker rather than silently leaving stale localized legal meaning in production.
