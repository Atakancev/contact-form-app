# TycoonX EU/German Influencer, Creator & Affiliate Marketing Release Gate

Last reviewed: **September 2, 2026**  
Owner: **CK-Labs**  
Scope: TycoonX creator sponsorships, influencer posts, affiliate links, creator promo codes, revenue-share campaigns, free or discounted entitlements supplied for promotion, reposted endorsements, testimonials, ratings/review campaigns, giveaways run with creators, and Xsolla Partner Network attribution.

TycoonX entered full release on **September 1, 2026**. Nothing in this gate describes the live service, users, purchases, VIP, Diamonds, rewards, or legal terms as beta.

## Purpose

CK-Labs may lawfully use creators, influencers, affiliates, referral partners, promo codes, revenue sharing, review prompts, and promotional collaborations to market TycoonX. The compliance risk is not the existence of creator marketing. The risk is hiding the commercial relationship, turning paid promotion into apparently independent opinion, pressuring children, misrepresenting prices or entitlements, buying or manipulating store reviews, using affiliate tracking without the required privacy/device-access basis, or allowing a creator to make promises that do not match the actual TycoonX product.

This is an operational release gate. It supplements, and does not replace:

- `TYCOONX_EU_PROMOTION_DARK_PATTERN_RELEASE_GATE.md`;
- `TYCOONX_EU_GERMAN_DIRECT_MARKETING_COMMUNICATIONS_RELEASE_GATE.md`;
- `TYCOONX_DSA_ADVERTISING_RECOMMENDER_MINORS_RELEASE_GATE.md`;
- `TYCOONX_TDDDG_TERMINAL_ACCESS_CONSENT_RELEASE_GATE.md`;
- `TYCOONX_EU_GERMAN_PRICE_PROMOTION_PERSONALIZATION_RELEASE_GATE.md`;
- `TYCOONX_EU_VIRTUAL_CURRENCY_RELEASE_GATE.md`;
- `TYCOONX_GERMAN_MINOR_PURCHASE_RELEASE_GATE.md`;
- the canonical TycoonX Terms, Purchases & Refunds Policy, Privacy Policy, and Community Standards; and
- Apple App Store, Google Play, social-platform, and Xsolla rules that apply to the actual campaign.

This gate does not create an influencer or affiliate program by itself and does not state that TycoonX currently operates one.

## P0 release rules

### 1. Classify the relationship by substance, not the label

Before giving a creator or affiliate anything of value or activating campaign attribution, record whether the relationship includes any of the following:

- fixed cash payment;
- revenue share or commission;
- affiliate link or referral commission;
- creator promo code tied to commission or other benefit;
- free or discounted Diamonds, 30-Day VIP, Lifetime VIP, game items, access, travel, event tickets, hardware, services, or another benefit;
- free content, media assets, or other consideration supplied with an expectation of promotional coverage;
- guaranteed placement or reposting;
- a barter arrangement;
- employee, founder, contractor, agency, or other relationship that makes the commercial connection relevant; or
- another direct or indirect economic benefit connected to the endorsement.

Do not avoid advertising rules by calling a commercial campaign `community`, `ambassador`, `friend of TycoonX`, `creator support`, `gift`, `collaboration`, `PR`, `partner`, `organic`, or `fan content` when the real arrangement is promotional.

Conversely, do not force an advertising label onto a genuinely independent player post merely because the person likes TycoonX, tags CK-Labs, or discusses a feature without receiving or being promised consideration and without another commercial connection. Preserve evidence when the distinction matters.

### 2. German UWG § 5a(4): commercial purpose must be recognizable

For Germany, UWG § 5a(4) makes it unfair to fail to identify the commercial purpose of a commercial practice when that purpose is not directly apparent from the circumstances and the omission can influence a transactional decision.

For an act in favour of another company, the provision currently says a commercial purpose does not exist merely from that act if the person receives or is promised no remuneration or similar consideration from that company. Receipt or promise of consideration is presumed unless the person credibly shows otherwise.

Operational rule for CK-Labs:

- if CK-Labs pays, gives a commission, supplies a promotional benefit, or otherwise provides value linked to the content, assume a clear advertising disclosure is required unless a documented legal assessment establishes otherwise;
- do not rely on the audience guessing that a relationship exists;
- do not rely on the creator's profile bio alone; and
- do not treat a private contract clause saying `creator is independent` as eliminating the real commercial purpose.

### 3. Hidden advertising and fake-consumer presentation are prohibited

German UWG Annex No. 11 prohibits paid editorial content used for sales promotion where the commercial connection is not clearly apparent from the content or its visual/acoustic presentation.

The EU Commission's UCPD Guidance treats influencer content broadly for this purpose and explains that commercial communications can include paid posts, affiliate links, discount codes, retweets, tags, free products, discounts, partnership arrangements, event invitations, trips, and other consideration.

Do not:

- pay a creator to present TycoonX as an independent personal recommendation without disclosure;
- operate a CK-Labs-controlled `fan` account that pretends to be an ordinary consumer;
- ask a moderator, contractor, employee, friend, or creator to hide their relationship while recommending TycoonX;
- repost a sponsored creator post onto an official TycoonX channel after stripping the advertising disclosure; or
- use screenshots or edited clips in a way that removes the original disclosure and makes the endorsement look independent.

UWG Annex No. 22 also prohibits falsely claiming or creating the impression that a trader is acting as a consumer. CK-Labs-controlled marketing must not masquerade as grassroots player opinion.

### 4. Disclosure must be visible where the audience actually sees the content

The EU Commission's current UCPD Guidance says the commercial disclosure must be clear and appropriate for the medium, considering placement, timing, duration, language and target audience. It gives examples of inadequate disclosure such as a label buried after a long disclaimer, merely tagging the brand, or requiring the consumer to click `read more`.

For a German-facing campaign, the current German media-authority guidance recommends a clear label such as `Werbung` or `Anzeige` and requires recognizability at first glance. Its current guide also says labels should remain visible across devices and app/browser views, without requiring scrolling or expansion.

Release baseline:

- label each commercial post, story, short, livestream segment, podcast segment, video, image carousel, or other communication individually where required;
- put the disclosure before or at the point the commercial content becomes visible or audible;
- do not hide it only in a creator bio, profile page, hashtag cloud, collapsed description, comments, or separate landing page;
- use language understandable to the likely audience;
- make the text sufficiently visible and persistent for the medium; and
- use the social platform's paid-partnership tool where helpful, but do not assume the platform tool cures a disclosure that is otherwise unclear.

A general statement such as `this channel sometimes contains ads` is not a substitute for labelling the specific commercial communication.

### 5. Affiliate links and promo codes need their own disclosure

The Commission expressly treats affiliate content as commercial communication where a creator receives commission. The German media authorities' current guide likewise treats affiliate links and discount codes as commercial and recommends that the explanation be placed in immediate proximity to the relevant link.

For TycoonX creator links/codes:

- disclose that the link or code is commercial and, where relevant, that the creator can receive a commission or revenue share;
- do not present an affiliate link as an independent `best price` comparison;
- do not hide the disclosure on a different page;
- do not imply that a promo code is official, exclusive, lowest-ever, or time-limited unless that claim is true for the actual offer; and
- do not imply that use of the code changes statutory rights, refunds, withdrawal rights, or store/payment-provider responsibilities.

### 6. Free Diamonds or VIP supplied for promotion are still valuable benefits

A non-cash benefit can still create a commercial relationship. The Commission's UCPD Guidance expressly includes discounts, free products, travel/event invitations and other non-monetary consideration when assessing influencer marketing.

If CK-Labs supplies a creator with free or discounted:

- Diamonds;
- 30-Day VIP;
- Lifetime VIP;
- an account upgrade;
- a promotional item;
- access unavailable to ordinary players; or
- another material benefit

in connection with promotional coverage, record the grant and require an appropriate commercial disclosure.

Do not describe such a grant as a normal paid purchase. Do not fabricate an Apple, Google Play or Xsolla receipt. Keep free/promotional grants distinguishable from purchased entitlements in operational records.

### 7. Lifetime VIP creator campaigns must preserve the real product

A creator may advertise Lifetime VIP only when the campaign accurately preserves all canonical limitations:

- Lifetime VIP is a one-time entitlement, not a subscription;
- it is available only during selected genuine promotional sales windows;
- CK-Labs may withdraw it from future sale;
- it may never return;
- prior availability creates no expectation of continuous or future availability;
- different genuine sales windows may have different future prices; and
- a later price decrease does not automatically create a refund, credit or price-match right for an earlier completed purchase, while a later increase does not create an extra charge on an already completed one-time purchase, except where mandatory law requires otherwise.

A creator must not say `always available`, `last chance forever`, `never coming back` or similar absolute language unless CK-Labs has actually made and can support that exact claim. `May never return` is not the same as a promise that it definitely will never return.

### 8. 30-Day VIP must remain one-time and non-renewing

Current 30-Day VIP is a one-time, non-renewing entitlement.

Creator scripts, video descriptions, promo pages and affiliate landing pages must not describe it as:

- a monthly subscription;
- auto-renewing VIP;
- a recurring €/$ charge; or
- a free trial that converts automatically into a paid subscription.

If CK-Labs later introduces a recurring product, that product requires its own compliant recurring-subscription launch and marketing treatment before a creator campaign is approved.

### 9. Diamond claims must match the actual bundle and checkout

A creator must not state or imply a Diamond bundle quantity, bonus, price, discount, expiry rule, transferability rule, refund rule, or regional availability that does not match the actual eligible checkout.

If a creator says `1,000 Diamonds for €9.99`, `20% bonus`, `cheapest way to buy Diamonds`, or `webshop price is lower than App Store`, the claim must have a documented, like-for-like factual basis for the relevant country, channel, currency, tax treatment, bundle and time period.

Creator marketing does not override the final total price presented before transaction confirmation.

### 10. Price, scarcity and countdown claims stay under the promotion gate

Influencer marketing is not an exception to ordinary promotion law.

A creator may not be instructed or allowed to use:

- a fake crossed-out former price;
- a manufactured `50% off` comparison;
- a countdown that resets or is not tied to a genuine campaign end;
- false `only 10 left` scarcity for a digital entitlement;
- an invented `lowest price ever` claim;
- a false claim that taxes or FX changes are a special CK-Labs discount; or
- a regional price from another country as though it were the viewer's current offer.

The evidence requirements in `TYCOONX_EU_PROMOTION_DARK_PATTERN_RELEASE_GATE.md` and the price/personalization gate remain controlling.

### 11. Children: no direct exhortations to buy or pressure adults

German UWG Annex No. 28 prohibits an advertisement from directly exhorting children to buy the advertised product/service or persuade parents or other adults to buy it for them.

The EU Commission's March 21, 2025 CPC position on Star Stable Online specifically identified concerns including direct exhortations to children, pressure through time-limited practices, unclear virtual-currency information, and failure to ensure influencers clearly disclose commercial content and avoid unduly influencing children.

Do not give a creator language such as:

- `Kids, buy Diamonds now`;
- `Ask your parents to get Lifetime VIP before the timer ends`;
- `Keep asking your parents until they buy it`;
- `You need VIP to be a real TycoonX player`; or
- another direct or manipulative child-focused purchase prompt.

If a creator's actual audience materially includes children or young teenagers, apply the child/vulnerability review even when the creator's nominal channel category is general audience.

### 12. Vulnerability and immersive-game pressure

UWG § 3(4) and the UCPD framework require assessment from the perspective of a foreseeable vulnerable group where appropriate. Influencers often have a trust relationship with their audience, and the Commission's guidance notes that this can increase the risk of undue influence, especially for children and young people.

Do not combine a creator's personal trust with:

- shaming users who do not spend;
- suggesting friendship/status depends on buying;
- repeated purchase nagging during emotionally charged gameplay;
- targeting known vulnerable users because they have already spent heavily;
- account-loss or reward-loss threats that are not real; or
- exploiting a person's refund, account-compromise, support, or financial difficulty to sell another product.

### 13. Testimonials must reflect a real and supportable experience

A creator can give a genuine opinion, but CK-Labs must not script or approve a false personal experience.

Examples:

- if the creator was granted Lifetime VIP yesterday, do not script `I have used Lifetime VIP for a year`;
- if the creator has never bought Diamonds, do not script `I always buy this bundle because it is the best value` as a factual personal statement;
- if access was free, do not present the entitlement as something the creator personally paid for unless that is true;
- do not claim guaranteed profits, guaranteed in-game success, guaranteed investment returns, or guaranteed status from a TycoonX purchase; and
- distinguish opinion from objectively verifiable claims about price, quantity, duration, odds, functionality or availability.

### 14. Never buy, fabricate or condition App Store/Google Play ratings

Creator marketing and store-review acquisition are separate activities.

Apple's current App Review Guidelines prohibit manipulating ratings, reviews, App Store discovery or referrals. Apple also says attempts to inflate chart rankings with paid, incentivized, filtered or fake feedback can lead to enforcement, including removal from the Developer Program.

Google Play's current policy prohibits manipulating placement by fraudulent or incentivized ratings/reviews and gives offering a benefit in exchange for a rating as a violation example.

Therefore, do not offer or instruct a creator to offer:

- Diamonds for a five-star review;
- VIP for any Google Play rating;
- a coupon only after a positive App Store review;
- giveaway entries conditional on leaving a store rating;
- compensation for deleting a negative store review; or
- bulk, coordinated, scripted or fake reviews.

A normal, non-incentivized request to leave an honest store review must still follow the applicable platform rules and review-prompt APIs.

### 15. Consumer reviews and social endorsements cannot be fabricated

German UWG Annex No. 23b prohibits claiming that consumer reviews come from actual purchasers/users without taking reasonable and proportionate verification measures when making that claim. No. 23c prohibits submitting or commissioning fake consumer reviews or endorsements and misrepresenting consumer reviews or endorsements on social media for sales-promotion purposes.

Do not:

- buy a package of positive TycoonX reviews;
- generate fake player testimonials with AI and present them as real users;
- create fake accounts to praise TycoonX;
- selectively edit a review so its meaning becomes materially more positive; or
- label an influencer testimonial as a `verified player review` unless the verification claim is supportable.

### 16. Reusing a real review or creator post requires rights and context

A lawful positive review is not automatically a free advertising asset.

Apple's current ratings/reviews guidance says customer reviews may be used in marketing materials only with permission from the reviewer. More generally, CK-Labs must also preserve applicable copyright, personality/image, trademark, privacy and contractual rights when reusing creator or player content.

Before reposting a review, quote, clip, image, stream excerpt, voice, likeness or music in a TycoonX advertisement:

- obtain the permission/license actually needed;
- preserve the commercial disclosure where the reused material came from a paid collaboration;
- do not materially alter the meaning;
- do not imply continuing endorsement after the relationship ended if that would be misleading; and
- do not reveal private account, payment, support or security data.

### 17. Creator contracts must not authorize unlawful claims

A creator agreement should clearly require, at minimum where relevant:

- truthful and legally compliant advertising disclosures;
- compliance with campaign briefing and applicable platform rules;
- no false claims about TycoonX features, prices, refunds, legal rights, scarcity or availability;
- no direct exhortation to children where prohibited;
- no fake ratings/reviews or review incentives;
- no unauthorized guarantees or warranties on behalf of CK-Labs;
- no representation that the creator is CK-Labs customer support, payment support or a regulator;
- no disclosure of confidential security, fraud or anti-cheat information;
- no use of unlicensed music, images, clips, trademarks or third-party content supplied by the creator;
- correction/removal cooperation when a materially unlawful or misleading campaign asset is identified; and
- an evidence-retention period proportionate to the campaign and potential legal claims.

A contractual indemnity or `creator is solely responsible` clause does not erase CK-Labs' own responsibility under mandatory law when CK-Labs engaged, controlled, benefited from or reposted the marketing.

### 18. CK-Labs needs monitoring and correction controls

The Commission's influencer guidance gives an example in which a trader was held responsible after reposting inadequately labelled influencer content and notes the importance of measures such as transparency instructions, education and control mechanisms.

For a material campaign, keep a proportionate review process:

1. provide a compliant campaign brief;
2. give the creator the required disclosure language/placement guidance;
3. review high-risk claims before publication where feasible;
4. spot-check live posts after publication;
5. capture evidence of the disclosure and claim as actually displayed;
6. notify the creator promptly if correction is needed;
7. pause links/codes or campaign traffic where a serious violation continues; and
8. document the corrective action.

Do not require pre-approval of every genuine creative opinion if not necessary, but retain stronger review for price, legal-right, child-directed, Lifetime VIP, Diamond, randomized-mechanic and other objectively verifiable claims.

### 19. Creator program fraud can be controlled without harming consumers

Affiliate and creator program terms may lawfully protect CK-Labs against fraud where drafted clearly and applied proportionately.

Depending on the actual program terms, prohibited conduct can include:

- self-referral designed only to generate commission;
- bot or fake-account traffic;
- cookie or attribution stuffing;
- fake purchases followed by coordinated refunds/chargebacks;
- stolen-payment traffic;
- unauthorized paid-search bidding on protected terms where the program forbids it;
- impersonation of CK-Labs or official TycoonX Support;
- deceptive domains or phishing;
- regional-price circumvention promoted as an affiliate tactic; and
- manipulating Xsolla attribution or creator identifiers.

A program can provide for withholding unpaid commission during a reasonable fraud investigation and reversing commission tied to refunded/reversed/fraudulent attributed transactions when the agreed program terms and applicable law allow it.

But an affiliate dispute must not itself:

- delete a legitimate customer's purchased Diamonds;
- restart or shorten the customer's original one-time 30-Day VIP;
- create an expiry in valid Lifetime VIP;
- manufacture a consumer chargeback or fraud finding; or
- remove a mandatory consumer remedy.

Consumer entitlement authority and creator-commission authority must remain separate.

### 20. Xsolla Partner Network attribution is not legally invisible

Xsolla's current Partner Network documentation supports creator revenue-share campaigns, tracking links, storefronts and promo codes. Its attribution documentation, last updated May 15, 2026, lists promo codes, token data, registration events and tracking links as attribution methods.

If CK-Labs uses Xsolla Partner Network:

- record the exact campaign type and creator benefit;
- make creator disclosure obligations part of the campaign brief/terms;
- keep the checkout offer and promo code accurate;
- keep creator payout/revenue-share logic separate from player entitlements;
- reconcile canceled/refunded transactions according to the actual Xsolla campaign configuration; and
- do not tell players that Xsolla sponsorship/attribution changes their statutory rights or the identity of the contracting merchant for the actual purchase.

Xsolla currently documents that creator-attributed purchases can be used to calculate revenue share and that canceled/refunded attributed purchases cease to be confirmed for creator payout purposes. That creator balance treatment must not be confused with a right to claw back unrelated player property.

### 21. Xsolla tracking links/localStorage trigger a separate German TDDDG check

Xsolla's current payment-attribution documentation describes a `tracking_id` and recommends a Partner Network script that can persist that identifier in `localStorage` so purchases can be attributed to a creator.

For Germany, deploying that script is not merely a marketing-operations choice. Storing or reading terminal-device information triggers the separate TDDDG § 25 analysis covered by `TYCOONX_TDDDG_TERMINAL_ACCESS_CONSENT_RELEASE_GATE.md`.

Release rule:

- inventory the exact cookie/localStorage/device access used by creator attribution;
- document whether § 25 consent or a statutory exception is relied on for each access;
- do not call optional affiliate analytics `strictly necessary` merely because it improves creator payout measurement;
- where consent is required, do not write/read the attribution identifier before the consent decision;
- respect withdrawal/refusal without deleting purchased entitlements; and
- consider a promo-code attribution path where technically and commercially suitable if it reduces unnecessary tracking.

A creator's disclosure that they earn commission does not substitute for TDDDG/GDPR consent or transparency where those rules independently apply.

### 22. GDPR and profiling remain separate from advertising disclosure

Commercial disclosure answers `is this advertising and who benefits?`. It does not answer `may CK-Labs process this person's data for attribution or profiling?`.

For creator/affiliate campaigns:

- map personal data and device identifiers actually used;
- identify controller/processor roles;
- apply GDPR transparency, legal-basis, minimization, retention and rights rules;
- apply the terminal-access gate to cookies/localStorage/device identifiers;
- do not turn an affiliate click into consent for email/push direct marketing;
- do not give creators raw player payment data, private email addresses, device fingerprints or support records merely to prove attribution; and
- do not use children or known-vulnerable users for profiling-based advertising where applicable rules prohibit it.

### 23. DSA advertising rules can apply to a different relationship

The external creator campaign covered here is different from TycoonX itself presenting a paid third-party advertisement on an in-scope online-platform surface.

If TycoonX later lets players or businesses buy promoted placement inside community feeds, art/music/book discovery, profiles or similar user-facing surfaces, run `TYCOONX_DSA_ADVERTISING_RECOMMENDER_MINORS_RELEASE_GATE.md` separately.

A creator disclosing `ad` on TikTok does not satisfy TycoonX's own Article 26 duties if TycoonX itself is separately presenting an in-scope DSA advertisement. Likewise, a DSA ad label inside TycoonX does not fix an undisclosed paid influencer post outside TycoonX.

### 24. Giveaways, prizes and creator promotions need real rules

If a creator runs a TycoonX-funded giveaway or promotion, record:

- organizer and sponsor roles;
- eligibility, territory and age limits;
- start/end time;
- entry method;
- prize identity and quantity;
- winner-selection method;
- notification/claim process;
- any platform-required disclaimer;
- whether taxes or reporting may apply; and
- how personal data will be handled.

Do not condition entry on an incentivized App Store/Google Play rating. Do not invent winners. Do not call a promotional grant a store purchase. Do not use a giveaway to pressure a child into buying or asking an adult to buy.

### 25. Provider outages, code expiry and campaign termination

If a tracking provider, social platform, Xsolla Partner Network or promo-code system fails:

- do not silently change the consumer's completed transaction price after confirmation;
- do not remove delivered entitlements merely because attribution failed;
- do not promise a creator commission that the governing campaign terms do not support;
- preserve enough transaction/campaign evidence to reconcile genuine disputes;
- make a reasonable correction where a valid advertised promo code failed because of CK-Labs/Xsolla configuration and mandatory law or the campaign terms require a remedy; and
- distinguish provider outage from consumer fraud.

If a campaign ends, old promotional content should be corrected or clearly contextualized where leaving it live would materially mislead consumers about a current price, availability or sales window.

### 26. Business sale or successor operator

A sale, merger, reorganization or successor operator does not automatically transfer every creator permission, marketing license, tracking consent, endorsement right or affiliate contract on broader terms than originally agreed.

Before continuing a creator campaign after an operator change:

- verify contract succession/assignment;
- update advertiser identity where needed;
- reassess privacy/controller information;
- preserve consumer-facing product truth;
- do not imply the creator endorses the successor beyond the scope of the agreement; and
- preserve valid player Diamonds, 30-Day VIP and Lifetime VIP according to their lawful entitlement state.

## Evidence packet for every material creator/affiliate campaign

Retain a proportionate package containing:

- creator/affiliate identity and channel(s);
- relationship and consideration supplied;
- campaign contract/accepted program terms;
- campaign brief and required disclosure wording;
- target territory and audience/age assessment;
- product/bundle/price/sales-window facts supplied to the creator;
- screenshots or recordings showing the live disclosure and material claim;
- affiliate link/code and attribution method;
- TDDDG/GDPR assessment for tracking where applicable;
- Xsolla campaign/configuration reference where used;
- moderation/correction history;
- payout/revenue-share calculation basis and refund/reversal treatment;
- content-reuse permission where CK-Labs republishes the creator material; and
- campaign close/end date and any corrective update required for content that remains online.

Do not retain unnecessary player payment or device data in the creator evidence packet.

## Release blockers

Do not launch or continue the affected campaign if any of the following is unresolved:

1. a paid/benefited creator post is materially presented as independent consumer opinion;
2. the required commercial disclosure is hidden, ambiguous or only visible after extra steps;
3. a child-directed campaign contains a direct exhortation to buy or pressure an adult to buy;
4. a creator is making unsupported claims about Diamond quantities/prices, 30-Day VIP, Lifetime VIP, refunds or mandatory rights;
5. a fake countdown, former price, scarcity claim or regional-price comparison is being used;
6. CK-Labs or a creator is offering an incentive for an App Store/Google Play rating;
7. fake reviews, fake player endorsements or fake `verified user` claims are used;
8. creator content is reposted by CK-Labs without the rights/permission required for that reuse;
9. Xsolla/other affiliate tracking writes or reads German terminal information before the required consent/exception analysis is satisfied;
10. creator payout logic can directly mutate player entitlements;
11. an affiliate can access unnecessary raw player payment/security/personal data;
12. a serious known campaign violation remains live without a correction/escalation path; or
13. the campaign materially changes the public legal meaning of a TycoonX product but the canonical and localized legal documents have not been synchronized.

## Regression scenarios

Test at least the relevant scenarios before launch and after material campaign changes.

1. **Cash-sponsored video**: clear commercial disclosure is visible before the promotional claim.
2. **Revenue-share link**: affiliate commission is disclosed near the link.
3. **Creator promo code**: discount and creator benefit are disclosed; checkout shows the correct final total.
4. **Free Lifetime VIP grant**: creator discloses the commercial benefit; no fake store receipt is created.
5. **Free 30-Day VIP grant**: creator does not call it a recurring subscription.
6. **Free Diamonds**: creator does not present the grant as a personal cash purchase.
7. **Unpaid genuine fan post**: not falsely forced into a paid-endorsement classification without evidence.
8. **Reposted sponsored post**: official TycoonX repost preserves the commercial context.
9. **Buried hashtag**: campaign fails if disclosure is only after `read more` or hidden among tags.
10. **Livestream**: disclosure remains understandable for users joining after the stream begins where the format requires it.
11. **Child audience**: no direct `buy now` or `ask your parents` exhortation.
12. **Lifetime VIP window**: creator states selected-window availability without promising continuous availability or certain permanent disappearance.
13. **Different regional price**: creator does not present another country's price as the viewer's current checkout price.
14. **Price decrease later**: old customer is not promised an automatic price match unless offered or legally required.
15. **App Store review request**: no Diamond/VIP/coupon incentive is attached.
16. **Google Play review request**: no benefit is conditioned on leaving a rating.
17. **AI testimonial**: synthetic quote is not presented as a real player's review.
18. **Xsolla tracking link**: attribution method and German terminal-access basis are documented before deployment.
19. **Xsolla promo-code-only campaign**: attribution works without unnecessary tracking where that model was selected.
20. **Refunded attributed purchase**: creator commission reconciliation does not delete unrelated player entitlements.
21. **Chargeback/fraud investigation**: unpaid affiliate commission may be investigated under program terms, while consumer rights and unrelated entitlements remain separately assessed.
22. **Provider outage**: failed attribution does not change a completed consumer price or delivered entitlement by itself.
23. **Creator relationship ends**: stale price/availability claims are corrected when leaving them live would mislead.
24. **Review reuse**: reviewer permission is retained before an App Store review is reused in marketing.
25. **Business transfer**: advertiser identity, contract succession and privacy roles are rechecked.
26. **Official CK-Labs account**: commercial purpose is not disguised as an independent fan account.
27. **Creator giveaway**: no rating incentive, fake winner or child purchase pressure.
28. **Localized campaign**: German disclosure wording is understandable to German viewers and is not hidden behind an English-only ambiguous abbreviation.

## Public legal wording trigger

This gate is operational and does not by itself require a change to the canonical Terms, Purchases & Refunds Policy, Privacy Policy or Community Standards.

Reopen the relevant canonical English document and then all affected localized documents in the required locale order if a creator/affiliate program materially changes the public legal relationship. Examples include:

- CK-Labs introduces a consumer-facing referral program with contractual rewards/conditions that players must accept;
- the Privacy Policy needs new material attribution/tracking disclosure;
- a new creator marketplace changes UGC/commercial-communication rules;
- the paid-product meaning of Diamonds, 30-Day VIP or Lifetime VIP changes; or
- a new recurring product is introduced.

Do not update 25 locales merely to describe an internal marketing-control process that does not change player-facing legal meaning.

## Founder-safe operating position

The protective position for CK-Labs is not `the influencer is responsible, so CK-Labs has no risk`. It is:

- use a clear written campaign brief and contract;
- disclose commercial relationships prominently;
- keep child-directed pressure out;
- keep prices and Lifetime VIP scarcity claims factual;
- never buy or incentivize store reviews;
- separate creator commission from player entitlement authority;
- minimize affiliate tracking and pass TDDDG/GDPR checks;
- preserve evidence of the live ad and offer; and
- correct material violations quickly.

That preserves marketing flexibility while avoiding hidden advertising, fake social proof, platform review manipulation, unnecessary tracking and overbroad consumer clawbacks.
