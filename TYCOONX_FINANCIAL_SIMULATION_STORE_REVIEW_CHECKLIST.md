# TycoonX Financial-Simulation Store Review Checklist

Last reviewed: August 27, 2026

TycoonX is presented in its canonical Terms as an entertainment and economic simulation game. Its in-game money, Diamonds, shares, companies, property, products, and other game assets are fictional digital game elements and are not real bank deposits, securities, investments, legal tender, cryptocurrency, stored-value money, or claims against CK-Labs.

This checklist prevents the simulation from being accidentally presented to Apple, Google, or users as a real financial service.

## P0: App Store positioning

Before the September 1, 2026 full release:

- Keep App Store metadata, screenshots, previews, onboarding, support text, and App Review Notes consistent with TycoonX being a fictional business/economic simulation.
- If showing stock, crypto, company, loan, bank, investment, trading, or portfolio-style screens, avoid wording that implies CK-Labs executes real trades, holds real deposits, offers real loans, manages real money, provides regulated investment services, or guarantees real financial returns.
- Do not describe fictional TycoonX assets as securities, investments, deposits, cryptocurrency, cash equivalents, or withdrawable balances.
- Do not promise that in-game profit, ROI, stock performance, interest, or virtual wealth has real-world monetary value.
- Keep the public legal statement that virtual assets cannot be redeemed from CK-Labs for cash except where mandatory law requires otherwise.
- In App Review Notes, explain that financial-looking mechanics are game simulation systems using fictional game data and do not connect users to a broker, exchange, bank account, investment account, or real-world lending service unless that statement actually changes in the future.

Apple currently states that apps used for real financial trading, investing, or money management should be submitted by the financial institution performing those services and have the necessary licensing/permissions. The goal of this gate is to make it clear that TycoonX is not accidentally represented as that kind of app.

## P0: Google Play Financial features declaration

Google requires all developers with apps on Google Play to complete the Financial features declaration, including developers whose apps do not provide financial features.

Before release:

- Complete or recheck the TycoonX Financial features declaration in Play Console.
- Base the declaration on the actual app behavior, not merely on game vocabulary such as “stocks”, “bank”, “loan”, “crypto”, or “portfolio”.
- If TycoonX remains a self-contained fictional simulation with no real-money management, real trading, real cryptocurrency wallet/exchange, real lending, real payment/wallet service, or personalized real-world financial advice, preserve evidence supporting the declaration selected in Play Console.
- If TycoonX later adds any real-world financial feature, connected brokerage/exchange functionality, real wallet functionality, real lending, money transfer, or financial advice, re-evaluate the declaration and all applicable licensing/policy requirements before enabling the feature.
- Make sure the Play Store listing does not contradict the declaration by implying real investing, real crypto trading, real money management, or guaranteed earnings when the app is only a game.

## P0: no cash-out / no real-value loop

- Do not create a path where purchased Diamonds or other paid TycoonX value can be converted into cash, transferable real-world monetary value, or a withdrawable prize without a separate legal/platform review.
- Do not allow fictional stock, crypto, casino, lottery, race, or chance-style mechanics to become a hidden real-money gambling loop through cash redemption or transferable value.
- If any reward ever becomes redeemable outside TycoonX, stop relying on the current “fictional simulation only” legal positioning and perform a new gambling/financial-services/payment review before launch.

## P1: user-facing wording consistency

The existing canonical Terms already provide the core legal distinction. Store metadata should use shorter, original wording consistent with it rather than copying another game’s disclaimer.

Recommended concepts to preserve in metadata where useful:

- TycoonX is a business/economic simulation game.
- Game money, Diamonds, shares, companies, and other assets exist only within the TycoonX simulation.
- They are not real-world securities, investments, bank deposits, cryptocurrency, or guaranteed cash value.
- TycoonX does not promise real financial returns.

Do not overload the App Store description with legal text. The dedicated Terms, Privacy, Purchases & Refunds, Community Standards, Custom EULA, and Impressum remain the authoritative detailed sources.

## Sources checked on August 27, 2026

- Apple App Review Guidelines, current June 8, 2026 version: apps used for real financial trading, investing, or money management should be submitted by the institution performing those services and have necessary licensing/permissions.
- Google Play Financial Services policy: financial products/services relate to management or investment of money and cryptocurrencies, and apps with financial features must comply with local rules.
- Google Play Financial features declaration: all published apps must complete the declaration, including apps that certify they do not provide financial features.
