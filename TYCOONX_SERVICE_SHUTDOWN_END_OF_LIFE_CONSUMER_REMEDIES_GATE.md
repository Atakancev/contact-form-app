# TycoonX Service Shutdown and End-of-Life Consumer Remedies Gate

Last reviewed: September 11, 2026

Status: implementation and commercial release gate. This document does not replace the canonical TycoonX Terms of Service or Purchases & Refunds Policy. English remains canonical. Localized legal documents do not need to be reopened unless the canonical player-facing meaning materially changes.

## Purpose

TycoonX may one day need to discontinue all or part of the live service because of a business decision, severe technical or security problems, legal or regulatory requirements, platform removal, provider failure, force majeure, or another legitimate reason. A permanent shutdown must be handled as a consumer-contract event, not merely as a server switch-off.

The canonical TycoonX Terms already state that CK-Labs may permanently discontinue all or part of the service for a valid reason, that advance notice will be provided where legally required and reasonably practicable, that online accounts and game benefits may end when the service ends, and that mandatory refund, price-reduction, termination, warranty, and other consumer remedies remain unaffected. The canonical Purchases & Refunds Policy likewise states that Lifetime VIP is tied to the commercial operating lifetime of TycoonX and preserves mandatory remedies.

This gate translates those principles into an operational standard. It must not be read as a promise of an automatic full refund in every shutdown scenario, and it must not be used to deny a remedy that mandatory law requires.

## Core legal position

For German consumer contracts concerning digital products, sections 327 et seq. BGB can apply to paid digital content and digital services and, in specified circumstances, to digital products supplied in exchange for personal data. Under section 327i BGB, a consumer can have rights to cure, contract termination, price reduction, damages, or reimbursement of futile expenses when the statutory conditions are met.

For a digital product supplied continuously, German law links conformity and update duties to the applicable supply period. Section 327f BGB requires conformity-preserving updates, including security updates, during the legally relevant period. Section 327m BGB permits contract termination in specified cases of lack of conformity, including where cure is impossible, refused, unsuccessful, or where it is apparent that proper cure will not occur. Section 327n BGB provides for proportionate price reduction and requires reimbursement of an overpayment without undue delay and, in any event, within 14 days. Section 327o BGB governs the consequences of termination and payment reimbursement. Section 327r BGB separately protects consumers against certain changes to continuously supplied digital products that more than insignificantly impair access or usability.

Directive (EU) 2019/770 provides the EU framework behind these rules. It requires remedies for failure to supply or lack of conformity and provides for price reduction or termination in qualifying cases. For continuing supply, the value and period affected by non-conformity matter when calculating a proportionate price reduction.

Therefore, a TycoonX shutdown clause must never be interpreted as: "CK-Labs may close the game at any moment, keep every payment, and consumers have no remedy." Equally, EU/German law does not create a universal rule that every historical TycoonX purchase must always be refunded in full whenever an online game eventually closes. The applicable remedy depends on the product, the contract, the promised or reasonably expected supply, the time and value already received, the circumstances of the shutdown, the payment channel, and mandatory law.

## Distinguish four different events

### 1. Temporary outage

A database outage, hosting incident, authentication failure, DDoS event, maintenance period, provider incident, app-store review delay, network failure, or short emergency suspension is not automatically a permanent shutdown.

CK-Labs may take reasonable emergency measures needed to restore security or service integrity. Extended or repeated outages can still create statutory remedies where the legal conditions are met. Calling an outage "temporary" does not prevent a consumer from relying on mandatory rights if supply or conformity has materially failed.

### 2. Feature discontinuation or replacement

Removing or replacing an individual TycoonX feature is different from ending the entire service. Feature changes must be assessed under the existing digital-product change rules, including section 327r BGB where applicable. A feature replacement must not be described as a total service shutdown merely to avoid change or conformity obligations.

### 3. Provider migration or business succession

Replacing a hosting provider, database, authentication system, payment processor, or infrastructure provider is not itself a shutdown. Likewise, a sale, merger, restructuring, asset transfer, or successor operator does not automatically extinguish valid paid entitlements.

Where TycoonX continues through a lawful successor or migrated infrastructure, CK-Labs should preserve or migrate valid authoritative entitlements where the contract and applicable law require continuity. Any transfer of personal data must have an appropriate legal basis and comply with applicable privacy obligations.

### 4. Genuine permanent service discontinuation

This gate applies when CK-Labs has made a sufficiently concrete decision that all of TycoonX, or a material separately supplied paid part of it, will permanently cease to be provided.

The shutdown date, affected products, affected regions or platforms, surviving functionality, transaction cut-off, support period, refund/remedy process, and data-retention consequences must be identified before commercial communications are finalized wherever reasonably practicable.

## Commercial stop-sale gate

Once a permanent shutdown is actually decided and sufficiently certain, CK-Labs must not continue selling TycoonX products in a way that is misleading about the remaining usable period.

Before or at the point a permanent shutdown becomes sufficiently certain, the responsible operator must assess whether to:

- stop new Lifetime VIP sales immediately;
- stop or restrict new 30-Day VIP sales where a purchaser would not receive the represented 30 days of usable access;
- stop, restrict, or clearly qualify Diamond sales where the remaining usable period or functionality would make the normal offer misleading;
- stop scheduled promotions, coupons, push notifications, Post Office promotions, countdowns, crossed-out-price campaigns, and "last chance" marketing that were created before the shutdown decision;
- update Apple App Store, Google Play, and Xsolla catalog availability where CK-Labs controls it;
- inform Apple, Google, Xsolla, or another merchant/payment provider where their action is required; and
- preserve evidence of the exact date and time the shutdown decision became sufficiently concrete, because that date can matter when assessing later sales and consumer expectations.

A genuine wind-down offer is not automatically prohibited, but it must be truthful. If a paid item will be usable only until a known shutdown date, that material limitation must be disclosed clearly before purchase where legally required. Lifetime VIP must not be sold after a concrete shutdown decision in a way that creates a false impression of a materially longer commercial lifetime.

## Product-specific shutdown treatment

### Purchased Diamonds

Purchased Diamonds do not expire merely because time passes. A permanent shutdown can nevertheless make them unusable because the TycoonX service no longer exists.

The response must distinguish:

- purchased Diamonds attributable to valid paid transactions;
- promotional, compensation, test, review, event, gifted, or otherwise free Diamonds;
- Diamonds already consumed in valid gameplay;
- Diamonds removed because the source purchase was refunded, reversed, charged back, fraudulent, duplicated, or invalid; and
- balances distorted by a technical error or exploit.

Do not automatically treat the visible Diamond balance as cash owed by CK-Labs. Do not automatically treat all remaining Diamonds as worthless with no possible remedy either. Evaluate any mandatory remedy against the underlying paid transaction, the amount/value actually supplied and consumed, the timing of shutdown, applicable consumer law, and the relevant payment channel.

If a refund or price reduction is required, use source-authoritative transaction records where available rather than converting an aggregate balance with an invented exchange rate.

### One-time 30-Day VIP

Current 30-Day VIP is a one-time, non-renewing entitlement. It must not be processed as a subscription during shutdown.

If TycoonX remains available through the full valid 30-day entitlement period, the entitlement may simply run to its normal expiry unless mandatory law requires another remedy.

If permanent shutdown prevents delivery of part of the represented 30-day period, assess the affected transaction for the remedy required by applicable law. Do not send renewal language suggesting that current 30-Day VIP auto-renews. Do not revoke unrelated Lifetime VIP, another valid VIP source, Diamonds, or unrelated purchases merely because one 30-Day VIP transaction is being refunded or corrected.

### Limited-time Lifetime VIP

Lifetime VIP is a limited-time promotional one-time entitlement available only during selected genuine sales windows. It may be withdrawn from future sale and may never return.

A valid Lifetime VIP purchase is tied to the commercial operating lifetime of TycoonX for the purchasing account. It is not a promise that TycoonX will operate forever and it is not tied to the biological lifetime of a person, owner, company, server, device, or provider.

That commercial-lifetime definition does not eliminate mandatory consumer remedies. The timing of a shutdown relative to the purchase, the disclosures made at sale, the value and duration actually supplied, the reason for discontinuation, the consumer's reasonable expectations, and applicable law must all be considered.

Never use the phrase "commercial operating lifetime" as a blanket justification to sell Lifetime VIP shortly before a known shutdown and then deny all remedies.

### Promotional and complimentary grants

A free, promotional, test, review, ambassador, compensation, or complimentary entitlement may normally end with the service subject to its disclosed conditions and applicable law. Such a grant must be kept distinct from a paid entitlement.

A shutdown cleanup must not overwrite authoritative paid entitlement provenance merely because the same account also received free VIP or promotional Diamonds.

## Refund and remedy decision model

For each potentially affected paid transaction, determine at least:

1. the product and SKU;
2. Apple App Store, Google Play, Xsolla, or other authorized source;
3. transaction/order identifier;
4. purchase and fulfillment timestamps;
5. purchaser and actual entitlement recipient, including gifts;
6. product duration or represented commercial-lifetime basis;
7. amount, currency, VAT/tax handling, and merchant shown to the consumer;
8. whether value was delivered, consumed, partially consumed, or remained unused;
9. whether the payment is completed, pending, refunded, partially refunded, reversed, charged back, or disputed;
10. the shutdown decision date and shutdown effective date;
11. any closure disclosure presented before that purchase;
12. any mandatory local consumer right;
13. the remedy channel controlled by Apple, Google, Xsolla, CK-Labs, or another contracting merchant; and
14. whether another valid entitlement source must survive the correction.

Never use one global SQL-style balance reset or entitlement reset as a substitute for transaction-specific legal reconciliation. This gate does not authorize any production database change.

## No double recovery, no unrelated clawback

Where a transaction is refunded or otherwise unwound, CK-Labs may correct the corresponding paid entitlement or virtual value where permitted by law so that the same paid value is not retained twice.

That does not permit removal of unrelated legitimate value. A shutdown refund for one purchase must not automatically erase:

- unrelated purchased Diamonds;
- earned or promotional value that is not legally part of the corrected transaction;
- another valid 30-Day VIP source;
- a valid Lifetime VIP from a different source;
- an unrelated Apple, Google Play, or Xsolla purchase; or
- unrelated gameplay progress unless permanent closure itself necessarily makes the online service unavailable.

## Apple App Store responsibilities

Apple processes App Store In-App Purchases and operates the App Store consumer refund process. Apple App Store Server Notifications can report a successful `REFUND` for consumable, non-consumable, non-renewing subscription, and auto-renewable subscription transactions. Apple also supports `REFUND_REVERSED`, which indicates that a previously refunded transaction was reinstated and that revoked content or services may need to be restored.

For a TycoonX shutdown:

- do not promise that CK-Labs can force Apple to approve a consumer refund;
- keep Apple transaction identifiers and authoritative entitlement provenance available for lawful reconciliation;
- process Apple refund/revocation information source-specifically;
- restore a source if Apple authoritatively reverses a refund and restoration is still meaningful or otherwise required; and
- coordinate catalog stop-sale and consumer support steps that CK-Labs controls.

## Google Play responsibilities

Google Play distinguishes refunding an order from revoking access. Its current developer tools allow refunds for one-time purchases and provide separate refund/revocation mechanisms for subscriptions. Current TycoonX 30-Day VIP and Lifetime VIP must not be silently treated as auto-renewing subscriptions simply because Google also documents subscription shutdown workflows.

For a TycoonX shutdown:

- use the actual configured Google Play product type and authoritative order/purchase token;
- do not create recurring-product semantics for current one-time VIP;
- keep refund and entitlement revocation decisions source-specific;
- ensure a refunded transaction is not granted again by a stale retry or restore path; and
- preserve unrelated valid purchases.

## Xsolla webshop responsibilities

Where Xsolla acts as merchant of record, the Xsolla entity shown at checkout or on the receipt may be responsible under its transaction terms for payment processing, transaction tax/VAT handling, fraud screening, refunds, payment disputes, and chargebacks. CK-Labs remains responsible for its own TycoonX entitlement delivery obligations.

Xsolla currently documents transaction-specific refund webhooks. A shutdown process must therefore reconcile provider-confirmed refunds against the exact local purchase source and retain enough transaction state to prevent a refunded order from being granted later through retry, migration, import, or recovery processing.

Do not tell a consumer that Xsolla involvement removes mandatory rights. Do not tell a consumer that CK-Labs controls a refund decision that legally or operationally belongs to the merchant/payment provider.

## Pending and delayed payments near shutdown

A pending order does not create a completed paid entitlement merely because checkout was opened or the user returned to the game.

For a payment that becomes valid after a shutdown announcement but originated before the applicable stop-sale cut-off:

- inspect the provider-confirmed transaction time and status;
- inspect the offer that was actually presented;
- determine whether meaningful fulfillment remains possible;
- avoid granting unusable value solely because an automated webhook says "paid";
- refund or otherwise remedy the transaction where required or appropriate under the governing channel and law; and
- make the process idempotent so retry events do not create a duplicate grant or duplicate refund.

## Chargebacks, fraud, exploits, and account compromise

Permanent shutdown does not convert fraudulent or invalid transactions into valid transactions. Likewise, the existence of fraud controls does not remove mandatory remedies for unrelated valid purchases.

Keep these concepts separate:

- a consumer remedy caused by failure or discontinuation of the service;
- a provider refund or chargeback;
- a fraudulent payment;
- a duplicated or erroneous entitlement;
- exploit-derived game value;
- account compromise; and
- Terms enforcement against a player.

A compromised account may require transaction and device investigation before entitlement correction. Do not punish a player merely because abnormal activity occurred during a security incident. Use reliable evidence and preserve mandatory rights.

## Force majeure and security emergencies

Force majeure, cyberattack, regional infrastructure failure, war, government action, platform removal, or another external event may explain why supply is interrupted or continuation becomes impossible. It can support emergency operational decisions where the contract and law permit them.

It is not a magic waiver of non-waivable consumer rights. If mandatory law creates a remedy because the digital product is not supplied or no longer conforms, a force-majeure clause must not purport to erase that remedy.

Security emergencies may justify temporarily disabling purchases, login, transfers, markets, or other functions while CK-Labs contains the incident. A temporary security containment step should not be prematurely communicated as a permanent shutdown unless that decision has actually been made.

## Notice and communication gate

Where notice is legally required or reasonably practicable, a permanent shutdown communication should state clearly:

- that the closure is permanent rather than ordinary maintenance;
- the affected TycoonX service, region, or platform;
- the effective date and time;
- whether new purchases have stopped;
- what happens to Diamonds, 30-Day VIP, Lifetime VIP, and other paid entitlements;
- how users can seek support or a mandatory consumer remedy;
- which refund route applies for Apple, Google Play, Xsolla, or another merchant where known;
- any deadline that actually applies;
- what happens to account and personal data; and
- any export or retrieval method for eligible user-provided non-personal content where required.

Do not use a fake countdown or misleading urgency to increase purchases immediately before closure. Do not call a shutdown a "maintenance period" to delay legally required information.

For material consumer information, localize the communication appropriately for affected supported audiences where required or reasonably practicable. The TycoonX brand must always be displayed exactly as `TycoonX`.

## Data and user-created content after termination

Service closure does not authorize indefinite retention or unrestricted reuse of personal data. Apply the TycoonX Privacy Policy, GDPR legal bases, statutory retention obligations, deletion schedules, security requirements, and data-subject rights.

Section 327p BGB also contains rules concerning certain non-personal content provided or created by the consumer after contract termination, including circumstances in which the consumer can request provision of qualifying content free of charge, without hindrance, within a reasonable time, and in a commonly used machine-readable format.

Before shutdown, map which TycoonX user-created content could fall within such an obligation. Do not assume every game-state row, leaderboard record, economy log, or co-created social object must be exported. Apply the statutory conditions to the actual data category.

Legal, tax, fraud, chargeback, transaction, security, and accounting records may need to be retained after game access ends for the period required or permitted by law. Keep such retention distinct from continued gameplay access.

## Successor operator and business transfer

If TycoonX is sold or transferred and continues under a successor operator, do not automatically classify that event as permanent discontinuation.

Before a transfer, assess:

- whether valid paid entitlements can and should migrate;
- whether the successor assumes the relevant consumer-contract obligations;
- whether any product or feature materially changes;
- whether a consumer notice or consent is required;
- privacy-controller or processor changes;
- transaction and entitlement record migration;
- Apple, Google Play, and Xsolla account/provider requirements; and
- remedies required for consumers who cannot lawfully or technically continue.

A corporate reorganization must not be used as a paper mechanism to erase valid paid entitlements while substantially the same TycoonX service continues.

## Provider replacement and unsupported platforms

If Apple, Google, Xsolla, a hosting provider, authentication service, or another vendor changes rules or becomes unavailable, first assess reasonable replacement or migration where feasible. Provider replacement alone does not automatically terminate a consumer's valid TycoonX entitlement.

If a specific platform or app version becomes unsupported while TycoonX continues elsewhere, assess whether the affected purchaser has a reasonable supported migration path and what mandatory law requires. Do not characterize a platform-specific discontinuation as a total game shutdown when the service remains available through supported channels.

## Evidence that must be preserved

For a planned permanent shutdown, preserve an auditable package containing at least:

- decision and approval timestamp;
- reason/category for closure;
- proposed and final shutdown date;
- stop-sale timestamps for each payment channel;
- product catalog state around the decision;
- promotion and checkout screenshots or equivalent records;
- Terms and Purchases & Refunds version in force;
- Lifetime VIP commercial-lifetime disclosure presented at sale where available;
- provider transaction and refund identifiers;
- source-specific entitlement records;
- refund/reversal/reinstatement events;
- customer communications and localized versions;
- provider notices and relevant merchant terms;
- support scripts and escalation instructions;
- data-retention and deletion plan;
- user-content export analysis where applicable; and
- legal basis for any category of purchase for which no refund or other remedy is provided after a disputed shutdown claim.

Do not rely solely on a mutable profile `vip` boolean or aggregate Diamond balance as historical proof of what a consumer purchased.

## Minimum regression and tabletop scenarios

Before any planned shutdown procedure is considered ready, test at least these scenarios:

1. A German user buys 30-Day VIP, and shutdown would occur on day 10 of the 30-day period.
2. A German user buys 30-Day VIP after CK-Labs has internally fixed a shutdown date but before the catalog is disabled.
3. A user has Lifetime VIP purchased years before a normal commercial end-of-life decision.
4. A user buys Lifetime VIP shortly before a concrete shutdown decision.
5. A Lifetime VIP promotion is already scheduled when shutdown is decided.
6. A user holds unused purchased Diamonds from a valid transaction.
7. A user holds only promotional/free Diamonds.
8. A user's Diamond balance contains both purchased and promotional sources.
9. A user consumed most purchased Diamonds before shutdown and disputes the entire historical purchase.
10. Apple issues a refund after shutdown and later sends `REFUND_REVERSED`.
11. Google Play refunds one purchase while another valid VIP source must remain intact.
12. Xsolla sends a refund webhook for a transaction already marked fulfilled.
13. A delayed Xsolla or store transaction becomes successful after the purchase catalog has been closed.
14. A chargeback arrives for a transaction already included in a shutdown remedy process.
15. An account-compromise investigation overlaps with a shutdown refund claim.
16. A hosting outage lasts several days but CK-Labs has not decided to close the game.
17. A payment provider exits a region but TycoonX remains playable there through another lawful channel.
18. Apple or Google removes TycoonX from one storefront while existing users can still access the service elsewhere.
19. TycoonX is sold and the successor can continue valid entitlements.
20. A successor cannot technically or legally honor one category of entitlement.
21. A user requests qualifying non-personal user-created content after contract termination.
22. A player deleted their TycoonX account before shutdown but retains a potentially restorable non-consumable provider transaction.
23. A player was lawfully terminated for serious abuse before shutdown and separately has a valid unrelated purchase dispute.
24. A closure announcement is translated and all rendered text uses `TycoonX` exactly.

## Release acceptance criteria

A permanent TycoonX shutdown process is not ready until all of the following are true:

- a responsible decision-maker and legal/commercial owner are identified;
- the shutdown is distinguished from temporary outage, provider migration, feature replacement, and business succession;
- new purchase and promotion behavior has been reviewed before the announcement;
- Apple, Google Play, Xsolla, and CK-Labs responsibilities are mapped;
- paid Diamond provenance is available at transaction level where needed;
- 30-Day VIP remains one-time and non-renewing throughout the process;
- Lifetime VIP uses the established commercial-operating-lifetime definition without claiming mandatory rights are waived;
- refunds, price reductions, terminations, restorations, and reversals are idempotent and source-specific;
- unrelated valid purchases are preserved during transaction correction;
- consumer communication does not mislead about closure, remaining usable time, or available remedies;
- personal-data retention/deletion and qualifying user-content obligations are assessed;
- transaction, tax, fraud, chargeback, and accounting records are retained as legally required;
- support can identify the correct refund/remedy channel for the transaction rather than giving a blanket response;
- mandatory EU/German and other applicable rights remain intact; and
- the 24 tabletop scenarios above have passed or any unresolved exception has a documented legal and operational decision.

## Current canonical wording assessment

As of September 11, 2026, the canonical English TycoonX Terms and Purchases & Refunds Policy already contain the essential player-facing meaning needed for permanent service discontinuation:

- TycoonX is not promised to operate forever;
- Lifetime VIP is tied to the commercial operating lifetime of the service;
- permanent discontinuation can end online access and in-game benefits;
- virtual items do not automatically become cash-redeemable solely because the service closes; and
- mandatory refund, price-reduction, termination, warranty, and other consumer remedies remain unaffected.

This gate therefore does **not** materially change the canonical legal promise and does not trigger a new 25-locale synchronization cycle. If CK-Labs later changes the canonical shutdown meaning, that affected canonical document must be re-localized in the established locale order.

## Founder-protection rule

The lawful founder-protective position is not to promise perpetual operation and not to promise universal full refunds. It is to define Lifetime VIP honestly, preserve the ability to end an unsustainable or impossible service for a valid reason, stop misleading new sales once closure is sufficiently certain, keep transaction provenance, apply remedies source-by-source, and preserve every non-waivable consumer right. That position is materially safer than either extreme of promising that TycoonX can never close or claiming that closure can never produce a consumer remedy.
