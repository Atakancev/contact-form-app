# TycoonX Permanent Shutdown / End-of-Service Release Gate

**Status:** release and contingency gate for CK-Labs  
**Last legal/source review:** September 4, 2026  
**Applies to:** TycoonX, including purchased Diamonds, one-time 30-Day VIP, limited-window Lifetime VIP, Apple App Store purchases, Google Play purchases, and the CK-Labs TycoonX webshop using Xsolla.

This gate is for a genuine permanent discontinuation of all or a material part of TycoonX. It is not a shutdown plan and does not imply that CK-Labs intends to discontinue TycoonX.

The canonical Terms already state that TycoonX may be permanently discontinued for a valid reason, that Lifetime VIP lasts for the commercial operating lifetime of TycoonX rather than forever, and that mandatory refund, price-reduction, termination, warranty, conformity, update and other consumer remedies remain unaffected. This gate turns that public wording into an operational checklist so a future shutdown cannot accidentally become an unlawful `all purchases are lost / no refunds` event.

## 1. Core legal principle

Permanent closure is **not** a magic contractual escape hatch.

A shutdown can affect CK-Labs' ability to continue supplying a digital service, the conformity of an ongoing paid entitlement, the value/use of purchased virtual currency, and the consumer's rights on termination. The exact consequence depends on the product, contracting merchant, purchase date, remaining supply period, reason for closure, applicable law, and what remedy the consumer is entitled to in the specific case.

Do not write or display any blanket statement such as:

- `all purchases are forfeited when the servers close`;
- `Lifetime VIP can end at any time without any remedy`;
- `force majeure means no refunds`;
- `virtual currency can never be refunded`; or
- `platform rules replace statutory consumer rights`.

The Terms may define Lifetime VIP by the commercial operating lifetime of TycoonX, but that definition does **not** waive a mandatory remedy that arises because of the circumstances or timing of closure.

## 2. German / EU digital-product classification

For German consumer contracts within BGB §§ 327 et seq., classify each affected paid product before deciding the remedy.

Relevant current checkpoints include:

- **BGB § 327d:** a trader required to provide a digital product must provide it in conformity.
- **BGB § 327m:** for a deficient digital product, the consumer can have a termination right in the statutory cases, including where proper cure is excluded, refused, unsuccessful, or the defect is sufficiently serious.
- **BGB § 327n:** where the statutory requirements are met, the consumer can choose price reduction instead of termination; overpayments must be reimbursed without undue delay and in any event within the statutory 14-day period described there.
- **BGB §§ 327o and 327p:** termination has statutory consequences for reimbursement/use and for content supplied or created by the consumer.
- **BGB § 327r:** modifications of a continuously supplied digital product beyond what is necessary for conformity require the statutory contractual basis, valid reason, no additional consumer cost, clear information, and, for more-than-insignificant impairment, the required durable-medium advance information and termination/remedy path.

Official current BGB text:

- https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__327d.html
- https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__327m.html
- https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__327n.html
- https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__327p.html
- https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__327r.html

Directive (EU) 2019/770 Article 16 likewise preserves trader obligations after termination, including reimbursement rules and treatment of consumer-created non-personal content:

- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32019L0770

### Shutdown classification rule

Do **not** assume that announcing a permanent closure converts every affected contract into a discretionary product change under § 327r. A closure that makes an ongoing paid digital service unavailable may instead engage non-supply/non-conformity and termination/refund consequences. Classify the real facts before selecting the legal path.

## 3. Product-by-product shutdown matrix

Before any public shutdown announcement, create a dated matrix for all live paid products.

| Product | Minimum closure analysis |
| --- | --- |
| Purchased Diamonds | Current purchased/unused balance, purchase channel, age of purchase, whether the balance can still be lawfully and meaningfully used before closure, withdrawal/refund status, provider refund route, and mandatory remedies if utility is lost |
| Free/promotional Diamonds | Offer-specific conditions, disclosed expiry if any, and whether a goodwill use-up period is appropriate; do not mislabel free value as purchased value |
| One-time 30-Day VIP | Remaining paid days per entitlement, closure date, whether the full purchased period can still be supplied, and any pro-rata/statutory remedy that applies |
| Lifetime VIP | Validity of the original purchase, original sales-window evidence, meaning of commercial operating lifetime, closure timing relative to purchase, reasonable expectations created by the offer, and mandatory remedies |
| Other paid entitlements | Product duration, remaining performance, contracting merchant, restore status, and statutory/platform refund path |

A permanent shutdown must **not** convert one product into another merely for administrative convenience. In particular:

- do not automatically convert Lifetime VIP into 30-Day VIP;
- do not automatically replace cash remedies with Diamonds;
- do not use free promotional value as a substitute for a mandatory monetary refund;
- do not remove unrelated paid entitlements because one transaction is refunded; and
- do not replay purchase events during migration/closure and accidentally grant duplicate Diamonds or duplicate VIP.

## 4. Stop creating new exposure once closure is actually decided

Once a binding internal decision to permanently close TycoonX has been made, or closure has become sufficiently certain that continuing to market ordinary long-duration value would be misleading, immediately review whether new paid sales must be stopped or changed.

At minimum:

1. freeze new Lifetime VIP sales unless continued sale is legally reviewed and the actual limited remaining service horizon is disclosed clearly enough to avoid a misleading lifetime/value claim;
2. stop any Lifetime VIP countdown, crossed-out price, scarcity claim, or `last chance` promotion that is not genuine;
3. stop or adjust 30-Day VIP sales if the service will not remain available for the full period represented at checkout unless the legally compliant offer/remedy structure has been deliberately designed;
4. stop selling Diamond quantities that consumers cannot realistically use as represented before the known end of service where continuing the sale would be misleading or otherwise unlawful;
5. disable new purchases before disabling the backend needed to validate, deliver, refund, restore, or reconcile existing purchases; and
6. preserve the exact timestamp and decision record showing when CK-Labs moved from contingency planning to an actual closure decision.

Do not use a future shutdown plan as an excuse to manufacture a fake `limited time` Lifetime VIP sale.

## 5. Notice plan

Where notice is legally required, contractually required, or reasonably practicable, use a coordinated notice plan rather than relying only on a disappearing in-app banner.

A closure notice should clearly identify, as applicable:

- the exact TycoonX service/function that will stop;
- the effective date and time;
- the last date on which new purchases will be accepted;
- what happens to purchased Diamonds;
- what happens to remaining 30-Day VIP time;
- what happens to valid Lifetime VIP;
- the applicable refund/withdrawal/remedy route by purchase channel;
- how users can request a copy/export of qualifying content or personal data;
- the support channel and support-availability period; and
- any mandatory termination, price-reduction, refund, objection, or other remedy.

Do not promise a notice period that CK-Labs cannot reliably provide. For emergency closure caused by security, illegality, platform action or infrastructure failure, record why shorter notice was necessary and preserve mandatory remedies.

## 6. Apple App Store closure controls

Apple's current App Store Connect guidance distinguishes removing an app/IAP from sale from terminating the developer's underlying online service.

Current Apple checkpoints as of September 4, 2026:

- Apple says that before removing an In-App Purchase from sale, developers should announce the intention to remove it and stop merchandising it **at least 31 days before removal**, so people can use consumable IAP, and should notify Apple; Apple states that it reserves the right to offer refunds for unfulfilled IAP.
- When an IAP is `Developer Removed from Sale`, Apple states that customers who already purchased it retain access and the transaction remains available through StoreKit/App Store Server API.
- Removing the app from sale does not itself shut down a server-backed service and does not erase CK-Labs' separate contractual/consumer obligations.

Official Apple sources:

- https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-in-app-purchases/set-availability-for-in-app-purchases
- https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/in-app-purchases-and-subscriptions/in-app-purchase-statuses
- https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/create-an-app-record/remove-an-app

### Apple shutdown sequence

Before a planned closure, where feasible:

1. stop Lifetime VIP and other inappropriate new IAP merchandising;
2. set a documented IAP removal schedule consistent with Apple's then-current instructions;
3. preserve App Store transaction IDs/original transaction identifiers and signed server records needed for restore/refund reconciliation;
4. keep receipt/server validation working long enough to reconcile late or restored transactions;
5. notify Apple where its current process requires/recommends it; and
6. do not tell a user that Apple `removed access` if CK-Labs actually shut down the server-side entitlement.

## 7. Google Play closure controls

Google Play currently provides controls to deactivate one-time-product purchase options, and the Google Play Developer API can process eligible refunds for in-app purchase orders.

Current Google checkpoints as of September 4, 2026:

- `monetization.onetimeproducts.purchaseOptions.batchUpdateStates` can deactivate purchase options so they are no longer available for purchase.
- `orders.refund` can refund an eligible in-app purchase/subscription order; Google's current API documentation states that orders older than three years cannot be refunded through that endpoint.
- A technical limitation of a Google refund endpoint is **not** itself a waiver of a mandatory consumer remedy. If the normal API cannot process an otherwise required remedy, route the case through the then-current Play Console/support/merchant process instead of declaring the claim invalid.

Official Google sources:

- https://developers.google.com/android-publisher/api-ref/rest/v3/monetization.onetimeproducts.purchaseOptions/batchUpdateStates
- https://developers.google.com/android-publisher/api-ref/rest/v3/orders/refund

### Google shutdown sequence

1. deactivate relevant new purchase options before the service becomes unusable;
2. retain `purchaseToken`, `orderId`, product ID, purchase state and entitlement-ledger linkage;
3. continue to process refund/void/chargeback state changes idempotently during the wind-down;
4. do not revoke unrelated Diamonds, 30-Day VIP or Lifetime VIP because another order was refunded; and
5. preserve a support route for valid older orders that cannot be handled by the normal developer refund endpoint.

## 8. Xsolla webshop closure controls

The Xsolla entity, merchant role, refund-policy type and transaction terms must be taken from the actual TycoonX project and transaction record.

Xsolla's current Refund Policy dated June 16, 2026 states, among other things, that:

- the contracting Xsolla company can depend on payment method and is shown during checkout/receipt;
- an item not received can qualify for refund under the applicable policy;
- a faulty/incorrect product can be subject to remedies according to the consumer's country law; and
- EU/EEA/UK withdrawal rights are addressed separately in the policy.

Current source:

- https://xsolla.com/refund-policy

For shutdown:

1. stop new Xsolla checkout availability before the underlying entitlement service is disabled;
2. preserve transaction number, merchant entity, refund-policy type, product, gross price, currency, tax and entitlement mapping;
3. coordinate refund/reversal handling with the transaction-specific Xsolla role rather than promising that CK-Labs can directly reverse every payment method;
4. continue consuming Xsolla refund/cancellation webhooks idempotently during the wind-down; and
5. do not state that `Xsolla is responsible for everything` where CK-Labs still has the developer/service obligation.

## 9. Purchased Diamonds at end of service

Purchased Diamonds require special care because they are not real-money stored value, but loss of the game in which they can be used can still have consumer-law consequences.

A closure plan must therefore distinguish:

- purchased and unused Diamonds;
- purchased Diamonds already spent/transferred/exchanged;
- free/promotional Diamonds; and
- invalid/duplicated/fraudulent Diamond grants.

Do not promise cash redemption of all remaining Diamonds merely because the service closes. Equally, do not use `Diamonds have no cash value` as a blanket answer to a legally valid refund, termination, price-reduction, withdrawal, conformity, or damages claim.

Where a use-up period is offered, it must be meaningful. Keeping the server nominally online while disabling the features needed to spend Diamonds is not a genuine use-up period.

## 10. 30-Day VIP at end of service

For every active one-time 30-Day VIP entitlement, preserve:

- purchase/activation time;
- authoritative end time;
- number of paid days supplied before closure;
- number of paid days that cannot be supplied after closure; and
- provider/merchant refund or mandatory remedy status.

Do not silently accelerate expiry to the shutdown date and treat the lost paid days as `used`.

## 11. Lifetime VIP at end of service

The canonical TycoonX meaning remains:

- Lifetime VIP is a one-time entitlement sold only during selected genuine promotional windows;
- it may be withdrawn from sale and may never return;
- it lasts for the **commercial operating lifetime of the TycoonX Service for the purchasing account**, subject to the Terms and mandatory law; and
- it is not a promise that TycoonX operates forever or for the purchaser's biological lifetime.

This definition is founder-protective because it avoids an impossible perpetual-service promise. It must not be used deceptively.

For a closure affecting Lifetime VIP, retain the original purchase date, amount, currency, region, sales-window marketing, checkout copy, merchant, restore record, and any evidence of what reasonable service duration/value was represented. A user who purchased shortly before an already-decided closure presents a materially different risk from a user who received years of service before an unforeseen closure.

If closure is already decided, the safest default is to remove Lifetime VIP from sale rather than rely on the commercial-lifetime definition to sell a long-term-sounding product immediately before shutdown.

## 12. User-created content after termination

BGB § 327p contains a distinct post-termination rule for **non-personal content supplied or created by the consumer** while using the digital product.

Where that provision applies, CK-Labs must not simply assume that every piece of user-created content can be deleted or retained without analysis. The statute contains defined exceptions and, for qualifying content, requires the trader on request to provide the content to the consumer **free of charge, without trader-imposed impediment, within a reasonable time, in a customary and machine-readable format**.

For TycoonX, classify at least:

- user-created art;
- books/text authored by the user;
- music or other creative works uploaded or created through the Service;
- public profile/description content;
- collaborative content; and
- content that has no utility outside TycoonX or is inseparably aggregated.

Personal-data access/portability/deletion rights remain a separate GDPR analysis. Do not confuse a GDPR export with the BGB § 327p non-personal-content handback duty.

## 13. Data protection during wind-down

Permanent closure does not mean `delete everything instantly` and does not mean `keep everything forever`.

Create a shutdown retention map distinguishing:

- data still needed to operate the wind-down and support/refund process;
- transaction/tax/accounting records subject to legal retention;
- evidence needed for fraud, chargebacks, disputes or legal claims;
- open GDPR rights requests;
- user-created content subject to a handback obligation;
- backups with documented expiry; and
- data with no remaining lawful purpose, which should be deleted or anonymized in accordance with applicable law.

Do not destroy transaction evidence while Apple/Google/Xsolla refunds or chargebacks are still pending. Do not retain the entire production database indefinitely merely because a few financial records need longer retention.

## 14. Security and infrastructure wind-down

A shutdown is also a security event.

Before deleting or decommissioning production systems:

1. freeze new paid exposure;
2. reconcile payment-provider state with the TycoonX entitlement ledger;
3. export immutable evidence required for tax/refund/dispute obligations;
4. resolve or transfer open support, privacy, refund and chargeback cases;
5. revoke or rotate provider credentials, webhook secrets, signing keys and administrator access at the correct stage;
6. remove public endpoints that no longer need to exist;
7. preserve legally required records in a restricted archive rather than leaving the full production stack online; and
8. document destruction of unnecessary copies/backups on the applicable schedule.

Do not turn off webhooks before the last purchase/refund/chargeback events are reconciled.

## 15. Force majeure and third-party shutdowns

A force-majeure event, Apple/Google account action, Xsolla restriction, hosting failure, cyberattack, government order, war, natural disaster or provider insolvency may explain why closure occurred and may affect the legal analysis.

It does **not** automatically mean that every consumer claim disappears.

Record:

- what actually happened;
- whether performance is temporarily or permanently impossible;
- what mitigation was attempted;
- which party controlled the failed component;
- whether a substitute provider was reasonably available;
- what paid performance had already been supplied; and
- what mandatory remedy remains.

CK-Labs may rely on lawful limitation/impossibility rules where they genuinely apply, but should never communicate `force majeure = no refund` as a universal rule.

### 15A. Current EU videogame-preservation policy checkpoint

The European Commission's response of **June 16, 2026** to the European Citizens' Initiative commonly known as `Stop Destroying Videogames` is a useful current boundary for TycoonX shutdown planning.

The Commission stated that, at that stage, it **could not propose a standalone legal obligation requiring videogames to remain playable after they stop being provided commercially**. It also emphasized that existing EU consumer law already protects consumers, including through pre-contract information about contract duration and termination conditions and remedies where digital content or a digital service does not conform with the contract or reasonable expectations. The Commission noted that consumers may be entitled to a proportionate refund. It announced engagement with consumers and publishers by the end of 2026 to explore improved industry standards.

Current source:

- https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-will-engage-industry-following-european-citizens-initiative-disabling-videogames

For TycoonX, this means all of the following:

1. do **not** describe EU law as currently requiring CK-Labs, merely because TycoonX is a videogame, to operate servers forever, publish server source code, hand over backend credentials, release a community-server binary, or create a permanent offline mode;
2. do **not** use the absence of that new standalone preservation duty as a `we may shut down with no remedy` argument; the existing contractual, German/EU digital-product, refund, conformity, termination, update, withdrawal and liability rules in this gate remain fully applicable;
3. before an actual closure decision is announced, review App Store, Google Play, Xsolla/webshop, website and in-game marketing for statements such as `forever`, `permanent access`, `always playable`, `never shuts down`, or another representation that could create an expectation stronger than the canonical commercial-operating-lifetime wording;
4. preserve evidence of the server-dependent nature of the affected service and what duration/termination information was actually shown to the consumer at the relevant purchase time;
5. if CK-Labs voluntarily provides an offline fallback, read-only archive, data/save export, community migration path or another preservation measure, define its scope accurately and do not accidentally promise indefinite security maintenance, hosting, compatibility or support unless CK-Labs intends to make that commitment;
6. do not release production secrets, personal data, payment credentials, anti-fraud logic, third-party licensed material or security-sensitive server components merely to create a preservation mechanism; privacy, security, intellectual-property and provider obligations still apply; and
7. re-check this policy area before any real TycoonX shutdown and again when the Commission's announced end-2026 industry engagement produces a new recommendation, standard or legislative proposal. A 2026 policy statement is a current checkpoint, not a permanent guarantee that the law will never change.

This section is intentionally founder-protective in both directions: it avoids inventing a perpetual-playability obligation that the Commission has not established, while preventing the same policy development from being misused to erase existing mandatory consumer remedies or purchase-specific promises.

## 16. No mass enforcement during closure

Shutdown refunds, migrations or corrections must remain transaction-specific.

Never use an end-of-service process to:

- mark every refund as fraud;
- suspend users merely because they requested a lawful refund;
- remove unrelated purchases after one provider reversal;
- erase a valid Lifetime VIP before its actual shutdown effective date merely to simplify accounting;
- convert legitimate balances into `fraud` or `promo` classifications; or
- use chargeback language to discourage statutory remedies.

Real fraud, exploit use, abusive chargebacks and duplicate/invalid entitlements can still be investigated and corrected under the Terms and mandatory law.

## 17. Minimum evidence package before closure

A planned permanent closure is not release-ready until CK-Labs has a dated evidence package containing:

- final closure decision and effective date;
- reason/category for closure;
- notice copy and channels;
- Apple IAP removal dates and current Apple guidance snapshot;
- Google one-time-product deactivation dates and current Play guidance snapshot;
- Xsolla store-disable date and transaction/refund configuration snapshot;
- active purchased-Diamond balance report;
- active 30-Day VIP remaining-time report;
- valid Lifetime VIP entitlement report;
- pending/refunded/charged-back transaction report;
- user-created-content handback procedure;
- GDPR/support case handover list;
- refund/remedy decision matrix by merchant/channel/product/jurisdiction;
- final entitlement reconciliation hash/export or equivalent immutable evidence;
- support closing date; and
- data-retention/destruction schedule.

This package is internal evidence. Do not publish player personal data, purchase tokens, full payment identifiers, security logs, or fraud signals.

## 18. Emergency versus planned closure

If TycoonX must stop immediately because continuing operation would be unlawful, unsafe, technically impossible, or would expose users/payment systems to serious risk, security and legal safety take priority over the normal planned sequence.

In that case:

1. stop harmful/unsafe functions immediately;
2. preserve evidence;
3. stop new purchases as quickly as technically possible;
4. notify Apple, Google, Xsolla and infrastructure providers where required;
5. communicate to users as soon as reasonably possible;
6. reconstruct entitlements from authoritative records; and
7. process mandatory remedies after stabilization.

An emergency can justify shorter operational notice. It does not authorize rewriting purchase history or waiving non-waivable consumer rights.

## 19. Canonical wording invariants

The public TycoonX Terms must continue to preserve all of the following:

- TycoonX is the only displayed game-brand spelling;
- TycoonX goes to full release on **September 1, 2026**, and current legal/player copy does not describe the live service or purchases as beta;
- purchased Diamonds are distinct from free/promotional Diamonds;
- one-time 30-Day VIP is non-renewing unless a different future product clearly says otherwise;
- Lifetime VIP is a limited-window one-time offering that may never return;
- Lifetime VIP lasts for the commercial operating lifetime of the TycoonX Service rather than promising perpetual operation;
- permanent shutdown does not automatically make virtual items cash-redeemable;
- mandatory refund, termination, price-reduction, conformity, update, warranty, withdrawal and other consumer rights remain unaffected;
- Apple, Google Play and Xsolla responsibilities remain transaction/channel specific; and
- a provider outage, platform rule, business decision or force-majeure event is not described as automatically eliminating mandatory rights.

## 20. Pass/fail rule

**PASS** only if a planned or emergency closure can be executed without accepting new misleading paid exposure, losing authoritative purchase records, prematurely destroying support/refund evidence, duplicating entitlement corrections, or relying on a blanket no-refund/force-majeure clause.

**FAIL** if the only shutdown plan is `turn off servers`, `remove the app`, or `the Terms say Lifetime ends`, without a product-by-product remedy, platform wind-down, content/data, security and evidence plan.
