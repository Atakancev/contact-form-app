# TycoonX EU/German Green Claims & Sustainability Marketing Release Gate

**Owner:** CK-Labs  
**Applies to:** TycoonX marketing, store metadata, web pages, webshop surfaces, social posts, influencer/affiliate briefs, email/push campaigns, in-game commercial banners, LiveOps promotions and public sustainability claims  
**Status:** P0 legal/commercial readiness before **27 September 2026**  
**Last reviewed:** 3 September 2026

TycoonX went to full release on **1 September 2026**. This gate is a post-release marketing/commercial control. It does not describe TycoonX as a beta and it does not change the meaning of existing paid entitlements.

## 1. Why this gate exists

Germany has enacted the **Third Act amending the Act Against Unfair Competition (Drittes Gesetz zur Änderung des Gesetzes gegen den unlauteren Wettbewerb), BGBl. 2026 I No. 43 of 19 February 2026**, implementing, among other things, Directive (EU) 2024/825 on empowering consumers for the green transition. The relevant green-claims rules apply from **27 September 2026**.

The rules tighten how businesses may use environmental claims and sustainability labels. They apply to commercial communications, not only to physical packaging. TycoonX therefore needs the same discipline across app-store metadata, a CK-Labs or Xsolla webshop, websites, social posts, influencer/affiliate campaigns, email/push messages and in-game promotional surfaces.

The safest founder position is simple: **do not make an environmental or sustainability claim unless CK-Labs can identify exactly what is being claimed, what evidence supports it, what scope it covers, and whether the required qualification is visible on the same medium.**

### Current primary references

- Directive (EU) 2024/825: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2024/825/oj/eng
- German legislative procedure / implementation overview: https://www.enorm.bund.de/SharedDocs/Gesetzgebungsverfahren/DE/2024_AendG_UWG_EmpCo.html

This is an engineering/commercial release gate, not legal advice for a disputed case. Mandatory consumer rights remain unaffected.

## 2. Scope: what counts as an environmental claim

Treat a statement, image, badge, product name, campaign name or implication as an environmental claim when it says or suggests that TycoonX, CK-Labs, a TycoonX product, a purchase, a campaign or part of the service:

- has a positive or zero environmental impact;
- is less harmful to the environment than another product, service, company or purchase;
- has improved its environmental impact over time; or
- has a particular environmental characteristic that can influence a consumer's commercial decision.

A claim can be express or implied. Green imagery, leaf symbols, badges, icons, product naming, crossed-out carbon figures or a campaign theme can contribute to the overall impression even if the copy avoids a specific legal term.

### TycoonX surfaces in scope

Audit at least:

1. Apple App Store product-page text, promotional text, custom product pages and screenshots;
2. Google Play store listing, promotional content and screenshots;
3. CK-Labs pages and support/legal pages where commercial claims appear;
4. the TycoonX webshop and Xsolla-hosted/custom-domain checkout/catalog surfaces;
5. Diamond bundle descriptions and promotional banners;
6. one-time 30-Day VIP descriptions and campaigns;
7. Lifetime VIP sales-window descriptions and countdowns;
8. email, push and in-game LiveOps announcements;
9. official social accounts, creator briefs, affiliate links and paid influencer posts;
10. press kits, landing pages, ad networks and retargeting creative.

A statement in a legal document is not automatically an advertisement, but do not use legal/support copy as a back door for promotional environmental claims.

## 3. P0 rule: no unsupported generic green language

From **27 September 2026**, do not publish a generic environmental claim to German/EU consumers unless the applicable legal standard is satisfied.

High-risk examples include:

- `environmentally friendly` / `eco-friendly`;
- `green`;
- `climate friendly`;
- `carbon friendly`;
- `ecological`;
- `good for the environment`;
- German equivalents such as `umweltfreundlich`, `klimafreundlich`, `grün`, `ökologisch` or similarly broad formulations.

Directive (EU) 2024/825 adds a prohibition on a **generic environmental claim for which the trader cannot demonstrate recognised excellent environmental performance relevant to the claim**.

### Safer drafting rule

If CK-Labs has a real, material and substantiated environmental fact, say the **specific fact** rather than converting it into a broad green conclusion.

Example:

- Risky: `TycoonX is an eco-friendly game.`
- More defensible only if true and evidenced: `Our contracted data-centre provider reports that 100% of the electricity used for this specifically identified hosting region during the stated period came from renewable sources.`

The second statement still requires evidence, correct scope, current dates and an assessment of whether the overall presentation is misleading. It does **not** automatically justify calling all of TycoonX, CK-Labs or a purchase `green`.

## 4. Same-medium qualification

A generic-looking environmental claim is not rescued by an explanation hidden elsewhere.

Where a claim depends on a specification or qualification, the specification must be **clear and prominent on the same medium** as required by the applicable rule and must be visible before the consumer acts on the claim.

Do not rely on:

- a Terms link;
- the Privacy Policy;
- a buried FAQ;
- a tooltip that is unavailable on mobile;
- an influencer bio link;
- fine print below a long checkout page;
- a separate press release; or
- a support response sent after purchase

to correct an otherwise misleading headline or badge.

A short mobile screen is not a reason to omit a material qualification.

## 5. Sustainability labels and badges

Do not invent or display a voluntary `green`, `eco`, `responsible`, `sustainable`, carbon or similar trust badge merely because it looks professional.

Directive (EU) 2024/825 adds to the always-prohibited list the display of a **sustainability label that is not based on a qualifying certification scheme or established by public authorities**.

Before CK-Labs displays a sustainability label, preserve evidence of:

- the scheme owner;
- the public-authority basis or qualifying third-party certification scheme;
- the publicly available criteria;
- the applicable product/business scope;
- the certification period;
- independent monitoring/verification where required;
- current permission to use the mark; and
- conditions for suspension or withdrawal.

A self-designed leaf, globe, `CK-Labs Green Choice` badge or similar mark must not be presented in a way that consumers could reasonably understand as an independent sustainability certification.

---

## 6. No whole-business claim from a narrow fact

Do not claim that all of TycoonX or all of CK-Labs is environmentally superior when the evidence concerns only one narrow activity.

Directive (EU) 2024/825 adds an always-prohibited practice for an environmental claim about the **entire product or trader's entire business when the claim concerns only a certain aspect of the product or a specific activity of the business**.

Examples:

- A renewable-energy statement from one hosting region does not automatically make `TycoonX climate friendly`.
- An energy-efficient employee laptop does not make `CK-Labs green`.
- A provider's corporate sustainability target does not become TycoonX's achieved environmental performance.
- A one-day environmental charity campaign does not make every Diamond or VIP purchase environmentally beneficial.

Scope the claim to the actual evidenced activity.

---

## 7. Carbon-neutral and offsetting claims

Do not claim that a TycoonX product or purchase has a **neutral, reduced or positive greenhouse-gas impact because CK-Labs bought offsets or carbon credits**.

Directive (EU) 2024/825 adds this product-level offsetting claim to the always-prohibited practices.

Examples to block when based on offsets:

- `carbon-neutral Diamond pack`;
- `climate-neutral 30-Day VIP`;
- `Lifetime VIP with zero carbon impact`;
- `this purchase is carbon negative`;
- `buying this bundle cancels out your emissions`.

Do not rename an offsetting claim as `compensated`, `balanced` or another euphemism if the overall consumer impression is still that the product itself has neutral, reduced or positive greenhouse-gas impact.

A truthful statement about a separate corporate contribution or purchased credit may still require careful legal review, precise wording and evidence. It must not be presented as proof that the TycoonX product itself has neutral or reduced emissions.

---

## 8. Future environmental-performance promises

Do not publish promises such as:

- `TycoonX will be net zero by 2030`;
- `CK-Labs will become climate positive`;
- `all servers will be 100% renewable next year`

unless the applicable legal requirements are met.

Directive (EU) 2024/825 treats an environmental claim related to future environmental performance as misleading when it lacks **clear, objective, publicly available and verifiable commitments** in a **detailed and realistic implementation plan**, including measurable and time-bound targets, relevant implementation elements such as resource allocation, and regular verification by an independent third-party expert whose findings are made available to consumers.

Founder-protective default: if CK-Labs does not need a future green promise, do not make one.

If one is commercially important, create an evidence file before publication containing at least:

- exact claim text;
- baseline and measurement method;
- target and deadline;
- scope boundaries;
- responsible owner;
- resources/budget;
- milestones;
- dependencies;
- independent verifier;
- public verification route; and
- withdrawal/correction plan if the target changes.

## 9. Provider claims do not automatically transfer to TycoonX

Apple, Google, Xsolla, hosting, CDN, authentication, analytics, email, AI or other providers may publish their own environmental statements.

Do not automatically convert those statements into a TycoonX claim.

Before using a provider fact in TycoonX marketing, verify:

1. the exact provider entity/service and region used by TycoonX;
2. the period covered by the provider statement;
3. whether the statement concerns operations, purchased electricity, renewable certificates, offsets, targets or something else;
4. whether the evidence applies to TycoonX's actual workload;
5. whether CK-Labs is permitted to reproduce the claim/logo;
6. whether the wording remains true after provider migration; and
7. whether a consumer could mistakenly understand a narrow provider fact as a whole-product environmental claim.

A provider outage, acquisition, infrastructure migration or rules change must trigger revalidation of any dependent environmental claim.

## 10. Diamonds, 30-Day VIP and Lifetime VIP

Environmental marketing must not blur the legal/product distinctions among TycoonX paid products.

### Diamonds

Diamonds are virtual currency/virtual items under the existing TycoonX commercial framework. If a Diamond promotion includes an environmental message, the normal Diamond quantity, price, tax, regional-pricing, bonus and refund rules remain unchanged.

Do not imply that buying more Diamonds produces a verified environmental benefit unless the claimed benefit is real, quantified where necessary and legally supportable.

### One-time 30-Day VIP

The TycoonX 30-Day VIP is a **one-time, non-renewing 30-day entitlement**. A green campaign must not accidentally describe it as a subscription or alter its paid duration.

### Lifetime VIP

Lifetime VIP is a **limited-time promotional offering available only during selected genuine sales windows**. It may be withdrawn from sale, may never return, and creates no expectation of continuous future availability.

An environmental campaign must not be used to create fake urgency, false scarcity or a misleading justification for a Lifetime VIP countdown. The normal promotion, price-history, future-price and mandatory consumer-rights rules remain applicable.

No environmental claim itself authorizes CK-Labs to:

- delete purchased Diamonds;
- create a negative Diamond balance;
- restart, shorten or extend a 30-Day VIP period;
- add an expiry to valid Lifetime VIP;
- manufacture a refund, chargeback or fraud event; or
- retroactively reprice a completed purchase.

## 11. `Plant a tree`, donation and cause-marketing campaigns

If CK-Labs runs a campaign such as `we donate €1 per purchase` or `one tree for every VIP sold`, treat the donation promise and the claimed environmental impact as separate statements.

Before launch, document:

- the exact recipient/partner;
- the amount or calculation method;
- eligible purchases and campaign dates;
- any cap or minimum;
- when the contribution is made;
- what happens after refunds/reversals;
- evidence of actual transfer/performance; and
- whether any environmental result is guaranteed or merely expected.

Do not imply that a donation automatically makes the purchased TycoonX product climate neutral, environmentally friendly or carbon negative.

If a campaign promise cannot be fulfilled because a provider/charity disappears or a force-majeure event occurs, stop or correct the advertising promptly and apply ordinary consumer-law remedies where required. Do not silently substitute a materially different environmental benefit if that would change the commercial claim.

## 12. Promotions, countdowns, regional pricing and coupons

Green claims do not replace the existing TycoonX promotion/price rules.

For every environmental promotion:

- the promotion must be genuine;
- countdowns must correspond to real sales windows;
- crossed-out prices and discount claims must remain lawful and non-misleading;
- regional prices may differ legitimately by country/platform/channel;
- taxes, VAT and FX changes must remain correctly presented;
- a later price decrease does not automatically create a refund, credit or price-match right;
- a later increase does not add an extra charge to a completed one-time purchase; and
- promotion/coupon abuse may be addressed proportionately without removing unrelated valid entitlements.

Never suggest that a higher regional price exists because a purchase is `greener` unless that statement is actually true, material and evidenced.

## 13. Influencers, affiliates and community amplification

A paid creator, affiliate or agency must not invent environmental benefits that CK-Labs itself could not lawfully claim.

For campaigns involving green/sustainability language:

- provide approved claim wording and mandatory qualifications;
- prohibit unapproved `eco-friendly`, `climate neutral`, `carbon neutral`, `green` or similar claims;
- require advertising disclosure separately from environmental substantiation;
- preserve the final brief and published content;
- correct material misinformation promptly; and
- stop reusing a creator asset when its supporting facts expire.

A player's independent comment such as `TycoonX feels eco-friendly` is not automatically CK-Labs advertising. But reposting, quoting, pinning, featuring or paying to amplify the statement can create a different commercial impression and requires review.

## 14. Localization rule for environmental claims

A translated environmental claim must preserve the same **scope, qualification, degree of certainty and evidence** as the approved source.

Do not make a localized version stronger for marketing impact.

Examples of prohibited drift:

- English `hosting provider reports renewable electricity for Region X` becomes German `TycoonX ist klimaneutral`;
- English `we aim to reduce emissions` becomes French `zéro émission`;
- English `donation to an environmental project` becomes Turkish `her satın alma karbonu sıfırlar`.

If a new environmental claim is inserted into canonical legal/player-facing text and materially changes a TycoonX legal document, synchronize the affected document across all 25 target locales in the required order and update `TYCOONX_LEGAL_LOCALIZATION_PROGRESS.md`.

This gate itself does **not** change the canonical Terms, Purchases & Refunds Policy, Privacy Policy or Community Standards, so it does not reopen the completed 100-document localization queue.

## 15. Apple, Google Play and Xsolla boundaries

Platform and payment-provider rules remain separate from German/EU green-claims law.

- Apple may review store metadata, screenshots and promotional claims under App Store rules. Apple approval does not prove German/EU advertising-law compliance.
- Google Play may review listing/promotional claims. Google approval does not prove German/EU advertising-law compliance.
- Xsolla may host or process a TycoonX webshop transaction as configured under the applicable agreement. Xsolla's checkout/payment role does not automatically substantiate CK-Labs environmental marketing.

A provider's own tax, fraud, payment, regional-availability or refund role does not make that provider responsible for a CK-Labs environmental statement unless the applicable contract/law actually allocates responsibility that way.

Preserve the existing separation among purchase confirmation, entitlement delivery, refunds, chargebacks, VAT/tax handling, failed/reversed payments, fraud screening and marketing claims.

## 16. Errors, stale claims and correction workflow

An environmental claim can become inaccurate even if it was accurate when first published.

Trigger re-review when:

- a hosting/provider contract changes;
- a region or infrastructure stack changes;
- a certification expires or is suspended;
- measurement methodology changes;
- campaign dates/caps change;
- a charity/partner changes;
- an environmental target is missed or materially delayed;
- a regulator/court changes the relevant interpretation; or
- evidence is discovered to be wrong.

If a claim is materially wrong:

1. stop the affected advertising where reasonably possible;
2. preserve the original claim, dates, audience and evidence;
3. identify affected channels and transactions;
4. publish a correction where legally/commercially appropriate;
5. honor mandatory consumer remedies where the misleading claim affected a transaction; and
6. do not fabricate or backdate evidence.

A catalog/configuration mistake can be corrected prospectively, but completed transactions remain subject to the final price/offer actually shown and mandatory consumer law. Do not use a green-claims correction as a pretext to confiscate unrelated paid entitlements.

## 17. Evidence packet

Before approving any material environmental claim, keep a dated evidence packet containing:

- exact approved claim and each localized version;
- screenshots/mockups showing context and qualifications;
- channel, audience and campaign dates;
- exact product/business scope;
- data source and methodology;
- provider source material and contract/service mapping, where relevant;
- certification/label documents, where relevant;
- independent verification, where required;
- approver and review date;
- expiry/revalidation date; and
- correction/withdrawal plan.

Keep only personal data that is necessary for the legitimate compliance purpose. Green-claims substantiation is not a reason for extra player surveillance.

## 19. Release decision

### PASS

Pass this gate when:

- there are no unreviewed environmental/sustainability claims on active TycoonX commercial surfaces;
- any active claims have current, scope-matched evidence;
- generic claims, sustainability labels, offsetting claims and future-performance claims satisfy the applicable rules;
- translations preserve qualifications;
- provider facts are not overextended into whole-product claims; and
- promotions and paid entitlements remain legally and technically separate from the green-claims workflow.

### BLOCK

Block or remove the affected campaign before **27 September 2026** when:

- a broad green claim lacks the required substantiation;
- a sustainability badge lacks a qualifying certification/public-authority basis;
- a product-level climate claim is based on offsets;
- a narrow provider fact is presented as a claim about all of TycoonX or CK-Labs;
- a future environmental promise lacks the required plan/verification;
- a translation materially strengthens the claim;
- the campaign uses a fake environmental urgency/benefit to sell Diamonds or VIP; or
- CK-Labs cannot reconstruct the evidence supporting a material claim.

Founder-protective default: **remove the marketing claim rather than inventing evidence or an unenforceable disclaimer.**

## 20. Mandatory-rights safeguard

Nothing in this gate limits or waives non-waivable rights under German/EU consumer, competition, digital-content, privacy, product-liability or other applicable law.

If an environmental marketing violation gives a consumer a mandatory remedy, that remedy applies notwithstanding TycoonX's normal no-refund or final-sale language. Conversely, the existence of this gate does not create an automatic refund, credit, price match or entitlement extension where the law does not require one.

TycoonX's authoritative server/store/payment records remain relevant to payment and entitlement reconciliation, but they do not override mandatory consumer rights or determine whether an advertising claim was misleading.
